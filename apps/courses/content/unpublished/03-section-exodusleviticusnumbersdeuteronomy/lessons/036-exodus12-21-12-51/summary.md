

**Summary: Exodus 12:1-12:20**

**Title:** **God institutes Passover as instructions for the Hebrews to avoid the death of the firstborn and to remember that night of deliverance annually forever.**

**Storyline Summary:**

- **Take a lamb on the 10** **th** **of Nissan, 4 days in advance**
- **Lamb must be male, year old, without blemish**
- **Cleanse the house of leaven**
- **Twilight of 14** **th** **, slaughter the lamb**
- **Blood on doorposts and lintel**
- **Roast it whole**
- **Eat all of it, burn the leftovers**
- **Eat unleavened bread**
- **Do not eat leavened bread**
- **Eat bitter roots**
- **Eat with your sandals on your feet, ready to go**
- **If you do, I will Passover your house, if you don’t, firstborn dies**
- **Keep festival for 7 days of unleavened bread, do not work on first and last days**
