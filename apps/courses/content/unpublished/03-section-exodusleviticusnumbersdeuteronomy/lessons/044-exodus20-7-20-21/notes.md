**Exodus 20:7 > 3: name of Yahweh in vain**

- THIRD PERSON YAHWEH: this commandment uses the name Yahweh, obviously since the point is to not use the name Yahweh in vain. **But a shift happens here between the first two commandments that were spoken in the first person,** (I am commanding this, no other gods before me), and **from here on out in the Ten Commandments it is third person**: the name of Yahweh instead of “you shall not use **my** name in vain.” Instead it says “You shall not use **the name of Yahweh your Elohim** in vain”. The text doesn’t tell us why it does this, and Yahweh often switches between first and third person and the angel of Yahweh throughout the Old Testament. One explanation is obviously since this commandment is about the importance of his name, we start using his name throughout the rest of the 10 commandments.
- IN VAIN: what does it actually mean to use a name in vain? **What does vain mean?** If you call someone vain, it means they are obsessed with their own appearance. But that’s not what vain means here. **Here it means “without meaning” or “ without the appropriate meaning” or “ to no effect” or “without the appropriate effect”.** Like in Ecclesiastes, when Solomon says vanity of vanities all is vanity. It’s not a word we use often, so a lot of translations translated as “meaningless meaningless, all is meaningless”. So to take the Lord’s name in vain **is to use Yahweh‘s name to no effect, or in a meaningless way, or too in effect that is not consistent with what his name represents.**
- UNFORGIVABLE SIN: he asays here that he will not forgive this sin. He will not hold him guiltless who takes his name in vain. Jesus also tells us that there is only one sin that God will not forgive: **blasphemy against the Holy Spirit**. So the two sins must be one and the same. **Whatever it means to “take the Yahweh’s name in vain” must be the same thing as blasphemy against the Holy Spiri**t, since Jesus says every sin will be forgiven the sons of man except one, and Yahweh says he will not forgive this one. **So this seems like a pretty important one to figure out.**
- RABBINIC UNDERSTANDING IS THE VAIN OATH: the Rabbi’s of the Talmud take this as meaning **you cannot swear using God’s name falsely, and also that you cannot swear to a pointless truth using God’s name**. So you cannot swear that stone is gold, but you also cannot swear that stone is stone since that is self-evident.
  - FENCE AROUND THE LAW: They extended this to avoid saying the name of God, Yahweh or Jehovah, at all. If you never say it, then you can’t accidentally swear by it incorrectly. Since this is the one thing God will not forgive, it’s not unreasonable to go out of your away to ensure it never happens. **Not 99.9999999% sure, but 100%.**
  - NEVER EVEN WRITE THE LETTERS: They extended this to include never even writing the four letters of God’s name for fear that **the Torah scroll might become worn out or accidentally thrown away which would then be throwing away the name of God.** Instead they write four underscore blanks, and they use the word Adonai (Lord) when reading Torah and Hashem (“the name”) in everyday speech.
  - YAHWEH TOLD US TO CALL HIM YAHWEH: While I admire the care and respect they give to the name of God, Yahweh specifically told Moses that his name Yahweh was to be used to identify him forever. **So in saying other things, like Hashem, or Adonai, or LORD, we are not doing what he said, which was to identify him as Yahweh.**
- OMG: so everyone’s question here is I’m sure “does this Commandment tell us that we cannot say ‘oh my God?’” Is that the one unforgiveable sin? I have been on both sides of this fence in my life, so I will try to do justice to each.
- DO NOT CARRY: Dennis Prager gave the most compelling answer that I have heard on this question. The Hebrew word that we translate here “take” is “nasa”. **It’s literal meaning is “to lift up”, “to raise”, or “to carry”.** It is one of the most common words in the entire Bible, and it is the word used to describe what Aaron does with his breast plate containing the 12 stones that represent Israel. It is often translated therefore as “carry”. **Ancient Hebrew had plenty of words for “speak” or “tell” or “say” but it didn’t use those words. It uses raise or carry.** And if you understand it as carry instead of take, it makes a lot more sense. Because in English, what does it even mean to take a name? It means to carry that name with you. So the command is, **thou shall not carry the name of the Lord in vain.**
  - SO WHAT IS THE INTENDED EFFECT OF YAHWEH’S NAME? When you are doing something and carrying the Lord’s name with you conspicuously, you have raised it up to carry it, it must not be meaningless or fail to achieve its effect. **So what have we learned about Yahweh so far in the old testament? Is the story of reality not summarized as a battle between good and evil?** If you carry the name of Yahweh, you must carry it to achieve the side of the Good. If you carry it and instead achieve evil effect, you have not only carried the name of Yahweh to no effect, but you have carried it to its opposite effect. **You have done evil in God’s name.**
  - EVIL IN THE NAME OF GOD: And **nothing besmirches the name of God more than doing evil in his name.** Nothing keeps non-believers from the saving truth of Christ more than when people do evil in God’s name. Just as **it argues against atheism when you learn how godless Communism and Socialism committed the greatest evil smankind has ever known**, it also argues against God when his followers do evil in his name. The evil of Islam in the name of Allah argues strongly that Allah is Satan and it argues strongly that he is not to be worshipped.
  - UNFORGIVABLE TO COMMIT EVIL IN GOD’S NAME: I am convinced this is the commandment and the sin that God will not forgive: when you carry his name to do evil. The blasphemy against the Holy Spirit that Jesus said was unforgivable was when those **high priests who carried the authority of Yahweh** in their society, saw the Holy spirit performing miracles before their very eyes, and **they tell the people that the healing of this man’s hand came from Satan**, they do evil while carrying the name of God. They directly prevent people from coming to Christ to be saved after witnessing his miracles, tainting the miracle by claiming it is from Satan.
- SO IS OMG OK? When I reached that conclusion, I decided that the one unforgivable sin could not possibly be accidentally saying “oh my God” when you were stressed or amazed. Doing evil in God‘s name, that makes sense as the one unforgivable sin. But what I didn’t fully appreciate until Ray Comfort pointed out the importance of reverence for God’s name was just because this commandment doesn’t mean saying “oh my God“ is the one unforgivable sin, **that does not mean that it is a great idea to use our English term for God so flippantly and meaninglessly**.
  - OTHER WRONGS OUTSIDE OF 10 COMMANDMENTS: There are many wrong things that are not mentioned in the 10 Commandments. We should in fact, revere and respect God so much that we do he hesitate to use his name in meaningless context. **Or worse, to use the word God to replace a curse word.** Using the name of Jesus or the name of God to curse at something is as disrespectful as it gets. As Ray Comfort says, **would you use your mother‘s name in place of the word for excrement, to curse? If you did, wouldn’t that be dishonoring to her?** How much more so is it to the perfectly good creator of the universe?
  - WE ONLY CURSE WITH GOD’S NAME: And I find it fascinating that there is no other name with which we do this in modern society. Nearly All of our exclamation are either “oh my God,” or “Jesus Christ” some derivative that we use to avoid saying God or Jesus: **gosh, golly, goodness, good grief, geez, gee whiz, whew lordy, lordy Moses, the list goes on….why?** **Why is it natural as humans in every language to involve some reference to God in our cursing and pointless nonsensical exclamations?** They are actually very few words you can even use. Like “wow,” “rats,” or “yikes.” The part of us that wants to bring God into it meaninglessly is not good. That is not the purpose of God’s name. So don’t do it. But I don’t think it is the same as doing evil in God’s name.
  - RED HERRING: I consider it a red herring to make the argument that “God” is not God’s name, “Yahweh” is. We don’t even know fully how to pronounce God’s name. **The point is are you referring to the being named Yahweh or not.** And the answer is, if you talk to anyone in English, and you say “God”, you both know who you’re talking about.
- THIS IS THE GOD WE FOLLOW: who only has one unforgivable sin: doing evil in his name. Carrying his name in vain. Blasphemy against the Holy Spirit. All the others he will forgive, which is absolutely amazing to the sinner who needs forgiveness. The only hope we have of conquering evil is for Yahweh’s good name to remain unspoiled.   **The God whose name saves the world from evil and meaninglessness, that is the God we follow.**

**Exodus 20:8-11 > 4: Sabbath**

- I WILL TEACH THIS AS IT SHOULD BE TAUGHT: not necessarily what I do myself. I decided that the Sabbath is a gift, made for man and not man for the Sabbath, so I will teach the gift of the Sabbath in the hope that everyone appreciates and appropriates the gift of the Sabbath commandment in their life.
- ABRAHAM JOSHUA HESCHEL: I’ve referenced Abraham Joshua Heschel’s book, The Sabbath, in earlier classes. To help introduce the depth of the Sabbath and its implications, I would like to read some sections of his book.

- SEVEN GROUPS CANNOT WORK: within the commandment to not work every seventh day, there are seven groups listed that cannot work. Seven is the number of completion or completeness and it also is the number of a contract or covenant, so this is sort of a covenant for all time with all the sevens.
  - FOREIGNERS CANNOT WORK: if you were a foreigner, would you consider this a blessing or a religious rule imposed on you? You should see it as a blessing. For the Israelite, God left them no loopholes. They couldn’t just force everyone else to work so they could rest on the Sabbath. The Sabbath was more than just you not working. You are not allowed to have anyone else work for you either. And between the two, you working or someone else working, it does seem worse for you to rest while you make someone else work.
  - ANIMALS CANNOT WORK: this nobody could have predicted. God specifically says animals have to rest too. Just as not abusing animals made it into the laws for humanity given to Noah, they make it into the Ten Commandments too. Of course, animals don’t work by themselves, so they would have still taken the human.
  - WHAT IF YOUR ANIMALS NEED WATER? There are exceptions to the work prohibition on the Sabbath, one being the preservation of life. If your animals need food and water, you definitely give them food and water. Cows needs to be milked. However, if you can prepare the day before to avoid having to do things on Sunday, like having a bigger water bowl, then you should do that.
- SERVANT AND SLAVE: the Hebrew word used here for male servant, and female servant is the same word for slave, “eved.” Some translations render it as slave. **So why doesn’t the 10 Commandments say you shall not have slaves?** Why does it say your slave must be allowed to rest once every seven days? We will find as we get into the rest of the Torah that what you think of as “slave” is indeed prohibited by the Torah. Servant or bondsman is more accurate to what the Torah is talking about. For example, the commandment not to steal is primarily a commandment not to steal a human, I.e. kidnapping. So slavery that involves kidnapping a person to force them to work for you is prohibited. **This commandment is not about the African slave in the 1800’s: that’s the kind of slavery that Yahweh has freed Israel from.** And even then, with the servant, what’s commanded here is not condoning slavery, it is saying you have to give everyone who works for you a break one day per week.
- ONLY COMMANDMENT WITH A REASON: why? Dennis Prager says it could be because it is the only commandment that is a ritual, and rituals in and of themselves do not make sense without some symbolic meaning imparted to it. So God says you rest because I rested.
  - DEUTERONOMY 5 REASON: Moses recounts the Ten Commandments in Deuteronomy 5, and they are nearly identical except for the reason given for the Sabbath. The Sabbath is still the only one given with a reason, which supports the “because it is the only ritual” view, but the reason is different. There, it is because you were slaves and now you’re free.
- SABBATH IS A MESSAGE TO THE WORLD: the most conspicuous “separateness” (holiness) of Judaism and Christianity in every society that has ever been is the Sabbath. **No other culture or religion has ever had such a thing.** It is a powerful statement to the world to voluntarily abstain from making money one day every week. The Greeks labeled the Jews as lazy even because of it.
  - SOJOURNER: imagine being a visitor to a place that really observes the sabbath? Nobody works, no fast food, no stores or gas stations. It would make an incredibly powerful impact on you.
- KEEP IT HOLY: so is working unholy and resting holy then? We said holy meant “separate in a direction”, so work is unholy? Nope. Six days you shall do all your work. Work is holy for six days, rest is holy for one.
  - SO WHAT IS “KEEPING IT HOLY” THEN? Rest is a component, but only rest is not a holy Sabbath. Studying scripture, going to church, eating with family, disconnecting (separating) from the world, these keep it holy. Keeping it holy requires work, just different work than the rest of the week. Pastor Jeremy works on the Sabbath, it’s his biggest day, but his “work” is keeping it holy and so it is what the commandment intends.
  - 1/60th OF THE WORLD TO COME: the rabbi’s in the Jewish Talmud claim that the Sabbath is just a glimpse of the world to come, specifically 1/60th. This lines up with Hebrews 4. The Sabbath is a shadow of the peace to come in the next world.
- REMEMBER: why does it say remember? It says remember the Sabbath to keep it holy. It could be because they already knew about this from the manna earlier, so it is not a new idea. However, Dennis Prager offers another possibility that the Sabbath is already intrinsically holy, so you need to remember that it is holy, you don’t make it holy yourself. Yahweh made it holy when he stopped creation for the seventh day. Our job is to remember that holiness, we are not the source of the holiness.
- JEWS TURNED SABBATH INTO A BURDEN: Over the years the Jews turned the Sabbath into a burden. Instead of making it a day of rest, it often became a day of pain. They missed the point of the commandment: you should have the _menuha_ of the Sabbath, separate from the concerns of normal working life. Instead, when Jesus healed a man’s hand before their very eyes, they set out to arrest him for violating the Sabbath. When his disciples were walking through the field they grabbed a few pieces of wheat to eat and were condemned for harvesting. Today, in Israel, if you go to a hotel on the Sabbath that caters to the Orhodox Jews, on the Sabbath the elevators stop on every single floor so that nobody has to “start a fire” by connecting the contacts in the elevator switches. Burden, not rest.
- WHAT DAY IS THE SABBATH? Is it Sunday? Is it Friday night to Saturday night as the Jews celebrate? There are people with such adamant views on this that denominations have split over it, Seventh Day Baptists, Seventh Day Adventists, Churhch of God, some considering those who worship on Sunday to be unsaved pagan abominations. Seventh Day Adventists, for example, said that having church on Sunday is the mark of the beast in the end times. Why do we worship on Sunday? Not because it is the Sabbath, but because it is “resurrection day” or “the Lord’s day” celebrating the day of the week that Christ rose from the dead.
- DOES THIS APPLY TO CHRISTIANS? Skip Heitzig had an excellent take on this. First, this is the only ritual in the 10 Commandments. Are there any other rituals considered binding on Christians?
  - JERUSALEM COUNCIL IN ACTS 15: In act 15, the church in Jerusalem, responded to the question of what rituals from the law of Moses should the Gentile churches follow? Circumcision is what brought the question to the forefront and they decided they did not need to be circumcised. The final answer from the council came from James: “Therefore my judgment is that we should not trouble those of the Gentiles who turn to God, but should write to them to abstain from the things polluted by idols, and from sexual immorality, and from what has been strangled, and from blood.” Neither the Sabbath, nor any other rituals, were in there.
  - ROMANS 14 TO EACH HIS OWN FOR RITUALS: **Read Romans 14:1-6**. That puts it pretty clearly. Let him who is convinced in his own mind follow as he sees fit, but do not quarrel over these ritual and cleanliness issues.
  - LET NO ONE DISQUALIFY YOU FOR SABBATH: **Read Colossians 2:8-23.** There is specifically tells you all of the former things well festivals and ritualistic commands about touching and tasting are not disqualifying anymore. **So that’s about as clear as you can get the Sabbath is not apply to Christians**. So if one of the 10 Commandments is no longer disqualifying, does that mean all of them are OK? No, the Sabbath is the only ritual commandment in the 10 Commandments. You will not find anywhere in the New Testament that says now you’re OK to murder like it says that the Sabbath is not disqualifying for you. Jesus did not defeat the elemental spirits of the world so that you could murder or commit adultery. He defeated them so the Sabbath does not need to be a burden to you. It can be the gift it was always intended to be.
  - TRY TO DISCERN GOD’S WILL: Paul gives us what I think is the guide here for whether the Ten Commandments apply. **Read Ephesians 5:1-21.** Try to discern the will of Yahweh. Do not be foolish but understand what the will of Yahweh is. There are a lot of “thou shalt not’s” in that section from Paul. Does Yahweh want me to rest and be holy one day out of seven? Yes. Does he want that to become a burden to me? No. Does he want me to use my opinion on ritual observances to cause dissensions with my fellow brothers and sister’s in Christ? No.
  - SABBATH FOR MAN: Like we talked about before, the 10 Commandments is showing us qualities of God. It’s showing us the things that matter to him. Even though there’s nothing in the new testament telling us to observe this Sabbath, and we just heard from Paul that you can either esteem one day separate from the rest or steam them all from the standpoint of the law, one day of rest and holiness. Every seven seems important enough to God to put in the 10 Commandments. So it is certainly worth shooting for while not becoming a legal burden. Our God is the God who takes out of slavery, he’s a god of freedom. Working seven days a week is slavery. It is a gift from him to us to rest. To find _menuha,_ to be lead by the still waters. So it’s not like you’re not saved if you observed the Sabbath on the wrong day (we don’t even know what the right day really is because calendars have changed too much historically), are you don’t observe it for a few weeks, but you have indeed lost something that Yahweh does not want you to lose. He cares about you and thought that peace was so important for you to find that it should be in the 10 Commandments. Our God commands both work and rest.
  - MY DREAM SABBATH: turn off the screens, nap, read a religious book, enjoy the sunset. stop thinking about work.
- THIS IS THE GOD WE FOLLOW: the one who wants us to work six days and rest on the seventh. He cares about our rest. He restores our souls. Thank you Yahweh for commanding us to rest every seventh day and keep that day holy.

**Exodus 20:12 > 5: Honor father and mother**

- SPLIT BETWEEN MAN, MAN AND MAN GOD: the first four Commandments are between men and God. The last five Commandments are between man and man. The fifth Commandment splits the two and serves as a transition. Your mother and father, our fellow human beings, but they are also elevated in a Godlike position over you. So how you choose to treat your parents is a reflection of how you choose to treat God.
  - SOBERING AS A PARENT: Dennis Prager pointed out that the parents of a young child really are in a godlike position to them. You control their food, they’re waking, they’re sleeping, their self image, their internal thought life, their interests, everything about them. Whether you like it or not. It is sobering to realize the position you are in. Do not take it lightly. Be the parent who is worthy of the honor commanded you by God. You can be the second best mother. Second best because I already have the best mother! So the only position available is second best…
- IT DOES NOT COMMAND LOVE: you are commanded to love your heavenly father, but you are not commanded to love your earthly mother and father. But you are still commanded to honor them. No matter how they treat you. If you had good parents, you were among the blessed of the world. If you did not, you are far from alone. But this commandment does not have a condition. You are to honor them regardless. It is in fact, the child of the awful parent to whom this Commandment is primarily addressed.
