**12:43-end > Passover rules**

- MIXED MULTITUDE: Given the mixed multitude with them, it is important that we establish how the rules apply to the strangers and to the Hebrews. God here will distinguish between the foreigner who does not intend to stay and the stranger who is sojourning among them staying with Hebrews and intending to stay.   **If you do not intend to stay with the Hebrews, to be a part of God‘s people, then you cannot eat the body of the lamb.**
- ONE LAW FOR STRANGERS AND HEBREWS: no one uncircumcised can eat the lamb. The one law for both strangers and Hebrews is that you must be circumcised. And what would it mean for a stranger to become circumcised? It means that they have made a commitment in their life to follow Yahweh, that they want to be a part of his covenant with the Jews, and they have demonstrated this commitment with a physical action in the physical world. **So it turns out that the chosenness of God is available to the unchosen if they want to become children of the promise.** And for the Hebrews, who refuse to keep the Passover, who choose not to eat the body of the lamb and be saved from the wrath of Yahweh, they shall be cut off from their people we learn later. **And so we learn that God‘s chosenness is not simply being a child of Abraham, but it is deciding to follow Yahweh.** And following Yahweh means accepting the gift of the lamb that saved them from the death that is God‘s just punishment for evil. There shall be one rule for the Hebrew and the stranger.
- NO BROKEN BONES AND STAYS SAME HOUSE: Why in the world would God make these rules? What does this have to do with anything?
  - PROTECTION STAYS IN THE HOUSEHOLD: the lamb died to protect the household. Its body must stay with the household that it is protecting. If it leaves the household, either it will not have the protective effect it was killed for (it would not redirect the angel of death away from a firstborn) and be effectively neutered, or it will have the protective effect but not on a household that has chosen to put their trust in Yahweh. The body and blood of the Passover lamb will not cover anyone who has not put their faith in Yahweh. This is why everything not eaten must be burned and never leave the household.
  - LAMB NEVER DECAYS: the body of the lamb must be burned if it is not consumed so that it does not ever experience decay. The leaven was cleared from the house already, and the yeast in the air would start decaying the lamb’s body if it were not burned. The body of the sacrifice must not be allowed to decay. The sacrificial lamb must never see the corruption of yeast which represents sin.
  - SAVED INTO A NEW LIFE OF FREEDOM: not only does the blood of the lamb save you from the judgment and consequences of sin, which is death at God‘s hand, but it saved the Hebrews into a new life of freedom from slavery. Everything changed for them after they put the lambs blood on their door posts. God was physically present with them. And for the rest of the Bible, God primarily identifies himself as the God of freedom the God who took the Hebrews out of slavery.
  - WHY NO BROKEN BONES? this one is less clear in context. Why leave the body whole during cooking? Why can’t you break a bone? Is the body going to be re-used for something else after the meal? One explanation could be that the “spotless unblemished” nature of the lamb was to carry through all the way to consumption. A lamb with a broken leg was not acceptable for sacrifice. But they had already removed the skin and blood and cooked it. So it’s blemished now. So why keep the body intact? And why make this an annual activity for every generation forever? We will see an answer to this soon….
  - THIS EVENT CREATES JUDAISM: just as there are seven days of creation, there are now seven days commemorating the creation of the Israelite people as a people. This event creates Judaism. And Judaism becomes about the process of moving out of Egypt and into Israel. It is the process of getting Egypt out of the people. Out of you. Egypt is death, bondage, and nature gods. Israel is life, freedom, and Yahweh. In some sense it is a journey that every Jew had to make through these rituals. We all have to escape Egypt. Because without Yahweh, civilization naturally becomes death focused, full of bondage, and worshippers of nature. Life and Liberty come only through Yahweh.

**Modern Passover**

- REMOVE LEAVEN: hide bread or cake in the house, kids search for it. Thorough house cleaning. Then ready for Passover.
- SEDER: Jews today celebrate this with a family dinner once a year. They have a ceremony that the family reads from a “Haggadah”, and everyone participates. There are fun things, like singing, hiding bread for the kids to find, etc. The kids even have parts in the ceremony: the youngest reads the questions, they find the hidden matzah, they sing at the end.
- AFIKOMAN: Body of the lamb is represented as matzah. It is literally broken and the larger piece, called the “Afikoman” (meaning “that which comes after”) is hidden from the Jews. The children of the Jews search for it (keeps them engaged), and they find it by the end of the meal, and they finish the meal by eating the matzah as the last thing so the taste lingers, just as the temple-period Jews ate the body of the lamb as the last thing so the taste lingers. So the post-temple Rabbi’s literally changed the Passover ritual to be one in which the body of the lamb is hidden from the Jews, but one day their children will find it and they will all eat of it and be redeemed.
- ELIJAH: a cup of wine is poured at the table and left untouched for Elijah who will one day come to hail the coming of the Messiah. At the end of dinner, the kids all run to the door and open it to look outside to see if Elijah is here yet to hail the coming of the Messiah.

You and I both would have been right out there sarcastically yelling at Moses about there not being enough graves in Egypt. Our entire lives were upended, we have no idea what’s going on, and now Moses and this cloud led us right into a trap.

**Exodus 14:15-end > It’s over. It’s finally over.**

- TRUST IN YAHWEH: it says now they believe in Yahweh. This is the Biblical kind of belief. Belief can mean either “belief that something exists” or “trust in something.” Biblical belief is the latter: all Egypt and all the Hebrews knew quite well that Yahweh existed after the 10 plagues. They didn’t have to see the Red Sea parting to know he existed. No, this kind of belief is that now they start to trust Yahweh. He said he would take them out of Egypt, and they didn’t believe (trust) him. Now they do.
- IMAGINE HOW THIS TRUST WOULD FEEL:
  - in the last couple of months, we went from being tortured slaves, to getting our hopes up, to getting tortured more, to watching in awe as plagues hit us, then in even more awe as we watched plagues hit only the Egyptians. Bugs. Hail. Darkness. Death. Now we’re rich, and we’re finally out of Egypt. In the desert. But where are we going? Emotional exhaustion from the roller coaster. Kids “are we there yet?” Who do I even talk to? Who’s my leader? Should they be my leader? Who’s their leader? What are we going to do for food? What if the Egyptians come after us?
  - Wait, why are we camped here with only one way in and one way out? What is that dust cloud in the distance? Oh no, oh no, oh no. It’s the Egyptian army! A scream rings out. Moses, were there not enough graves in Egypt that you brought us here to be slaughtered? We’re doomed! There’s no escape! We are dead. Kids are screaming, “Dad, what do we do?! What’s going to happen?! Dad!?” I don’t know kids. I don’t know! We’re all gonna die! 2 million screams.
  - Wait, the pillar is moving toward the Egyptians. What is going on? What is Moses saying? Your leader says “Moses says stand by, Yahweh will take care of this!” Sure he will. Moses is just standing there with his arms up. The wind picks up. Oh no, we’re trapped between a storm and an army! We’re dead. Whoa, the wind is blowing so hard there’s a path through the sea! Has anyone on Earth ever seen anything like this? What?!? I can’t hear you over the wind! There’s a path! Run! Run kids! Come on donkey, move!
  - You make it to the other side. Oh no, the cloud is moving! The army is coming! They’re charging. Kids are screaming again, “Dad, what do we do?! Dad?! You’re screaming, “Moses, what do we do?!” but nobody can hear over the wind. They’re coming! I thought we escaped! Oh no, we’re doomed!
  - Suddenly, the wind dies. Instantly. A 100-foot wall of water crashes in a massive tidal wave over the chariots, the horses, the enemy. The water crashes over your certain death. You feel the spray, and you hear the screams. And then you hear nothing. Dead silence. The water is still. The wind is dead still. There’s Pharaoh, standing on the other bank, and between you is a huge expanse of glass.
  - We were dead, and now we’re alive. We were trapped. Yahweh saved us.
  - Israel saw the great power that Yahweh used against the Egyptians, so the people feared Yahweh, and the believed in Yahweh and in his servant, Moses.
- THIS EVENT CREATES JUDAISM: just as there are seven days of creation, there are now seven days commemorating the creation of the Israelite people as a people. This event creates Judaism. And Judaism becomes about the process of moving out of Egypt and into Israel. It is the process of getting Egypt out of the people. Out of you. Egypt is death, bondage, and nature gods. Israel is life, freedom, and Yahweh. In some sense it is a journey that every Jew had to make through the rituals instituted on the way out of Egypt. We all have to escape Egypt. Because without Yahweh, civilization naturally becomes death focused, full of bondage, and worshippers of nature. Life and Liberty come only through Yahweh. As slaves to sin, if you repent and put your trust in Jesus, then you have his promise that you will see the sea of glass after the tsunami waves crashes over the sin that pursues you.
