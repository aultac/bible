**Passover and Jesus:**

- ONLY OLD TESTAMENT USED UP TO NOW: I have not used any reference to the New Testament up to this point in the plague narrative, except for Revelation 16. Realize that everything we’ve learned has been Old Testament-only. Now let’s consider how the Passover relates to Jesus:
  - 10th OF NISSAN: Jesus chose to enter Jerusalem on the 10th of Nissan, 4 days before Passover. He chose this, he was not forced into the date. He presented himself to the high priests at the temple just as the passover lamb was presented 4 days before Passover.
  - 14th OF NISSAN: Jesus was crucified on Passover. On the commemoration of the exact day when Yahweh took the first born sons of Egypt and saved the Hebrews, the Hebrews finished his plan of taking Yahweh‘s first born. **On the night of Passover, the first born of Yahweh had died.**
  - 15th OF NISSAN: But his body would not see corruption or decay. None of its bones were broken. None of its limbs dismembered. None of the body was taken out of the household, except the blood on the wooden post of the cross, including the cross member, just as the lamb of the Passover sacrifice had its blood put on the wooden posts of the door, including the lentil above it. Unlike the firstborn, sons of Egypt, who died and stayed dead, the first born son of Yahweh was both first born son and lamb, the body and the blood. So this first born son is resurrected.
  - NO LEAVEN: leaven is a sign of decay. It’s what happens to dead things. No leaven is allowed in the house during Passover, because Jesus’s body never saw decay. It’s also universally a symbol of sin in the Bible. Jesus never sinned, the Passover lamb is spotless and leaven is not even allowed in the house that could contaminate it.
  - NO BROKEN BONES: now that we know the Passover prefigures Jesus, it is a direct Jesus fractal, we can see the answer we couldn’t see before. Jesus never had his bones broken. His body was never dismembered and carried outside the household, outside the tomb. His body remained intact. Why? Because he was going to be resurrected. While God could have made him a new body, he chose instead to decree that his original body would remain unbroken and intact. They never broke his bones, even though that was what they did to the two thieves on the crosses beside him. When he was resurrected, his body still had the holes in his hands and feet and the hole in his side. If they had broken his bones or dismembered him and scattered his body parts, that could not have happened that way. He could not have been resurrected in the same body that bore all the scars because he couldn’t walk on a broken leg and he can’t be missing an arm. This seems to me why God make this rule for this annual, eternally-decreed ritual. This rule exists at Passover precisely because it is necessary for the resurrection of Jesus the way he wanted to do it.
  - EAT THE LAMB WITH YOUR SHOES ON YOUR FEET: just as today we are supposed to take communion with our lamps trimmed ready for Jesus at any moment.
  - ONLY THE DEATH OF THE FIRST BORN REDEEMS US: the Passover is the culmination of 10 total plagues. Nine of them did not work to redeem God‘s people from the gods of Egypt. **Only the death of the first born can do that.** And even then, they are only saved from death by eating the whole body of an unblemished young male lamb and putting his blood on their door posts. We are learning a profound truth about God in reality here. How often do you hear people say if God would just do miracles, I would believe in him. But of course, that is the kind of belief the demons have: they know he exists. God is not interested in that kind of belief. He is interested in saving faith. And we learn here that nine of the most amazing miracles that you can imagine enacted on an entire country on an entire people were insufficient to both redeem and save. God is teaching us that our intuition that miracles will solve the problem is not correct. The Egyptians knew that he was Yahweh, pharaoh knew that he was Yahweh, even Hebrews knew by this point that he was Yahweh. Miracles do that, but they don’t bring the kind of belief that gets you to stop following the regional gods or the gods of your own construction, or your god of science and nature. That takes the death of a lamb, eating its body, and washing your household in its blood.
  - PASSOVER IS REQUIRED: **Read Numbers 9:9-13.** The penalty for failing to observe passover was that you would be cut off because you would have to bear your own sin. If you reject the Passover lamb that God has provided, the path that he gave you if he saved from death, then you will bear your own sin, and you have a committed a sin just because you failed to accept the path that God gave you for salvation.
  - REMEMBER THE BREAK IN THE DRAMA? Now we see why. Why was the most important part of this story that the Jews remember the details of Passover forever? Why was that more important even than their freedom? It’s because this one ritual passover tells you everything you need to know about Jesus. Their job was to keep that story alive until Jesus came, so that we would know that this had been God’s plan all along.
  - He had a plan to bring a savior to save the entire world who had to come through somebody. So he picked a broken vessel, a feral cat, to help show the world that he loves us and wants us to be saved.
  - WHY PLAGUES ARE FIRST INTRODUCTION TO HUMANITY: for the same reason you don’t tell non-Christians that Jesus is the savior before you make them aware that they are sinners in need of a savior. The Egyptians did not think they were bad. We don’t think we’re bad. God’s first a God of punishing evil, and then a God of salvation and forgiveness. Salvation and forgiveness are contingent on having something to be saved from and something to be forgiven for.

**Missing Passover: Judaism and Islam**

- JEWISH PASSOVER:
  - NO LAMB: the lamb’s actual body and its actual blood is conspicuously absent. The lamb’s body is not confined to the house. It’s not selected 4 days in advance. It’s not slaughtered at Twilight. The remains are not burned. There’s just a shank bone on a plate. That’s it.
  - NO BLOOD: there is no lamb’s blood on doorposts or included in the meal at all.
  - CHANGED WHEN TEMPLE WAS DESTROYED: Torah later prescribes that the lamb must be presented to the priests for inspection and slaughtered in the Tabernacle or Temple as a sacrifice, then cooked and eaten in the house. When the temple was destroyed in 70AD, about 40 years after Jesus’s death, the Rabbi’s were stuck with no place to sacrifice the lamb. So they removed the lamb from the Passover.
  - JEWS HAVE COMPLETELY FORGOTTEN SIN REQUIRES BLOOD: most modern Jews do Passover and fast on Yom Kippur as repentance for sin and think they’re covered with God. Jewish evangelism often needs to start with reminding them of the Passover and the sacrificial system. God never told them blood wasn’t necessary, but after the temple destruction they just decided it must be enough to just be sorry for your sins and keep the non-sacrificial laws.
  - AFIKOMAN: remember the middle piece of bread they break and hide? Turns out, that middle piece of bread represents the lamb. Its body is represented as unleavened bread that is broken and hidden from the Jews during the meal. The descendants of the Jews, the children, hunt and find it and bring it back to the Jews and then they all eat it to finish the Passover meal. The body of the lamb is bread that is broken, but the Jews can’t see it until the Passover is complete. Sound familiar?

**Exodus 13:11-16 > You owe your firstborn to Yahweh**

- CONSECRATE FIRST BORN: you don’t get to fully keep your first born. They belong to God. The first born were to perform the religious duties for God, but God changes this requirement in numbers chapter 3:11-13. Only the Levites are allowed to be priests at that point, and then in numbers chapter 18 God tells them they can buy their firstborn children back from him, redeemed them, for five shekels. This practice continues to this day
  - WHY DOES ISRAEL OWE HIM THEIR FIRSTBORN? As Mike Winger put it, God is saying “Israel, you owe me a firstborn.” Why do they owe their firstborn forever? God’s justice demands it for sin because he punished some sinners (Egypt) and not others (Israel). Every firstborn must die because the Egyptian firstborns all died and we are all sinners, so **it would not be fair for God to punish some sinners and not others.**
  - REDEEM OPTION: But God does not want humans to kill all their firstborn even though justice demands it. So he offers them an out: someone can redeem you. Someone else can buy the firstborn back from God in lieu of him or her actually having to die. The firstborn animals were sacrificed, and a lamb can take the place in death as a sacrifice for an animal like a donkey. Something can be a substitute death on the firstborn’s behalf.
  - GOD IS NOT EXEMPT FROM HIS OWN LAW: he also gives his firstborn son to Yahweh, just as all the Israelites have done since the Exodus. But we didn’t give him the option to buy back his son for 5 shekels. We didn’t say “ok, we’ll stop sinning, he doesn’t need to die. Just give us 5 shekels and we’ll stop sinning.” No, he had to go through with it all the way to death.
  - EVERY HOUSEHOLD HAD TO SACRIFICE THE FIRSTBORN: so we see that even God’s household required the sacrifice or redemption of the first born. God’s household has also achieved this protection from death by the sacrifice of his first born. Now the entire household of God is saved. And you can be a part of that household simply by putting your trust in him. You don’t have to follow a bunch of rules, or do a bunch of rituals, or marry into the right racial group, or move to live in Yahweh’s territory. All who repent and trust Yahweh to save them will be a part of his household, and will be saved. But no one outside the household of Yahweh will be protected by that blood.
