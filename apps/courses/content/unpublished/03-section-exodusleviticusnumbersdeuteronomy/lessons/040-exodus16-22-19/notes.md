- REVIEW: MAN DOES NOT LIVE BY BREAD ALONE: The advantage of how Moses laid out the Torah is we get Deuteronomy (“retelling”) to help explain things like this. **Read Deuteronomy 8:1-10.** The purpose of the Manna was to teach Israel and everyone who came after that man does not live by bread alone but by every word that comes from the mouth of Yahweh. **That purpose has now been achieved, so don’t expect manna to start falling from heaven again so you have something to eat when you are hungry.** The original purpose was not primarily to feed them, it was to teach them this. Jesus uses this to refute Satan when he is hungry and Satan tells him to turn stones into bread so he can eat.
- WHY DOES MANNA ROT IF YOU STORE IT? What is God trying to teach us here? The obvious first reading is they are going to need to depend on God every day. God did that intentionally. But we know from the New Testament that Jesus is the bread of life sent down from heaven, he is a fractal with the manna.
  - HOARDING MANNA MEANT DISTRUSTING GOD: When the Israelites used the bread from heaven against God’s explicit instruction, to hoard it up for themselves, so they wouldn’t have to trust God in the future, **the blessing from heaven became a plague.** The blessing that fell from heaven becomes a plague that fell from heaven, just as plagues fell from heaven on the Egyptians. Delicious food became bugs, decay, and stench.
  - MISUSING JESUS: Just as today, if you try to use the bread of heaven from Jesus selfishly and against the will of God, so that you do not have to have faith in God, Jesus’s body and blood will become a plague. **Those who choose to reject the bread from heaven that God offered so graciously become double sinners: sinners they always were, but now they are sinners who rejected the free gift of God.** And so they will decay, and become infested with sin, and stink to heaven.

And this should not be surprising: he seems to have the power to just put up his hands and win a war, raise a stick and part the sea, strike a rock and get water. These are the visual things that people actually see when God‘s miracles are done. Because they can’t see God‘s hand because if you saw it, part of God, you would die. **So it makes perfect sense that the people who left a land whose king was parked God, would expect that now their leader, who was doing greater miracles than any human had ever witnessed on earth, is also at least part God.** But Moses is the anti-Pharaoh: Moses constantly responds with the admonition that he is not God, and it is not by his power that these things are done except the numbers with the strikes the rock one time and does not give God the credit. And that keeps him out of the promised land.

**Exodus 17:5-7 > Water from Rock**

- PASS BEFORE THE PEOPLE: When God told Moses to walk before the people to strike the rock, it had two potential meanings: to prove to Moses this they wouldn’t stone him (he was perhaps exaggerating), and to prove to the people that God is taking care of them through Moses.
- STAFF WITH WHICH YOU STRUCK THE NILE: when he struck the Nile, he turned it to blood and it became undrinkable. When he struck the rock, it poured forth water to a thirsty people as their salvation. Same staff, opposite effects.
  - SCIENCE DOESN’T WORK HERE: it struck me this week that this specifically shows that the “staff” wasn’t some kind of magical Occult object. Spells and such things like that treated the spiritual world scientifically: you experiment, find a pattern that works consistently, then that becomes the incantation. This was all the magicians works in Egypt. But here, the same guy with the same staff does basically opposite things. Can’t experiment and draw conclusions. Yahweh is outside nature.
- ROCK WAS CHRIST: 1 Corinthians 10:4: “For they drank from the spiritual Rock that followed them, and the Rock was Christ.” Later, a sword strikes Jesus’s side, who was the rock, and water flowed out. From Christ, the Rock that followed them int he wilderness, pours the living water, and those who drink his blood and eat his flesh will never thirst or hunger again. Fractal with Moses striking the rock.

**Exodus 18:1-12 > Jethro and Zipporah visit Moses**

In our day, our Justice system has been completely the neutered because we have forgotten that most often, Justice and compassion are opposites. The compassion is not sell. Compassion to one can be cruelty to another. **Exodus 19:1-6 > Yahweh’s conditional promise to make Israel priests**

- FULFILLMENT OF THE SIGN: **Read Exodus 3:10-12.** They are finally here, at Horeb (Sinai). God fulfilled his sign.
- EAGLE’S WINGS: proof that not everything God says is literal, sometimes it’s poetic. He did not fly them out of Egypt on an eagle. The ancient Jewish Rabbi Rashi from around 1100 AD wrote this phrase comes from the fact that an eagle carries its young on its wings instead of in its talons because it knows it is king of the birds and nothing can fly above it, so it protects the young that way. Turns out, this doesn’t seem to be true. **Eagles don’t carry their young on their wings, Rashi was wrong**. God is just saying “I flew you out on a big bird’s wings”, which has a better poetic picture than “I dragged you out with a big bird’s talons”.
- REMINDER OF CARE BEFORE THE LAW: God is about to give them the 10 commandments and the law. But before he does that, he reminds them of how he took care of them.
- CONDITIONAL COVENANT: this covenant is preceded with a condition: “If you will indeed obey my voice and keep my covenant….” So if they do not, they have broken the covenant and Yahweh can void it.
  - UNCONDITIONAL COVENANT: Commentators say this is the first “conditional” covenant, whereas the Abrahamic covenant was “unconditional”: God said he would give Abraham many descendants, they would inherit the promised land, they would be sent to Egypt as slaves but come back to the land. Back in Genesis 15, God passed through the halves of the animals in that weird ceremony and signed his name to the contract, and it was re-iterated in Genesis 17 (inclusio). Then, after Abraham was willing to sacrifice Isaac in Genesis 22, the angel of Yahweh confirms that “because he obeyed my voice”, he will surely do what he said and added that the descendants will be a blessing to the nations of the world.
  - WAS THAT REALLY UNCONDITIONAL? The angel of Yahweh doesn’t tell us what would have happened if he withheld Isaac, but he does seem to indicate it was “cemented” when he listened to the voice of Yahweh.
  - CIRCUMCISION: But most importantly, in Genesis 17, God explicitly puts a condition on the promise.
  - CUT OFF: Esau was not child of the promise, and 19 things later can get you “cut off”
  - UNCONDITIONAL IN GENERAL, CONDITIONAL IN SPECIFIC PEOPLE: the promise is unconditional in general: it happened to “Israel.” But a particular descendant had conditions always. They could be a child of the promise, or they could reject Yahweh and be cut off from their people, no longer a child of the promise.
  - SEVEN PROMISES IN EXODUS: these promises were not made with explicit conditions: he said he would redeem them take them out of Egypt and give them the promised land. But again there’s a difference between the nation and the specific people. The nation of Israel, this happens even though they do not hold up their end of the bargain. But the specific people who escaped Egypt do not make it to the promised land except for Caleb and Joshua. **So the unconditional part of the promise is in general, the circumcision, obedience, and primarily trust in Yahweh are required to keep from being cut off from that promise.**
- CHOSEN FOR WHAT? Yahweh tells them he chose them to be his treasure and to be priests to the nations. He does not tell them that he chose them because they were amazing, he tells them he chose them to do something for him. He rules the whole world, and their job is to bring the knowledge of him to the rest of the world. They are to create the conduit by which the world and interact with Yahweh. That is what a priest is supposed to do Colon. They bring the request of the people to God. The profit goes the other direction: they bring the messages of God to the people.
- TENSION WITH HOLY PRIESTS: Dennis Prager points out that there is an implicit tension in what God tells Israel he has chosen them for. They are supposed to be a holy people, which means separate from the pagan nations around them, but they are also supposed to be priest to those nations. So there will forever be in the rest of Jewish history, constant shifting forward and backward between separateness and involvement with the nations to which they are supposed to be priests. The more they close themselves off to stay holy and perform God‘s rituals, the more the people around them distrust and hate them, and do not allow them to be their priests. But the more they engage with the communities, they are supposed to serve, the less separate, and holy, they become, their children getting absorbed into the culture around them.
- NEXT STEP IN GOD’S PLAN TO DEAL WITH SIN: this creation of a nation of priests is the next major phase in God’s plan to deal with the sin that Adam and Eve brought into the world. Remember how we got here: first, God made it easy not to send. The only made one rule. We broke it, and in breaking it, we got the next attempt to deal with sin: the conscience. Now we have an internal feeling or a little voice in our heads to make sure we know Wright from wrong. But we can overrule it, we can mute it, we can rationalize it away. With the conscience, the second brother kills the first brother. Eventually, the whole world is so bad all their thoughts are evil all day long and God wiped them all out with the flood, except for the best man in his generation, Noah and his family. Now phase 3, is that all people will be descended from the genes of good man. He ends up, passed out drunk and naked, not long after the flood, and curses his grandchild Canaan forever. Good genetic stock doesn’t seem to work. Now we receive the culmination of phase 4: one nation will act as priests the other nations, to give everyone on earth a conduit to God.
- REVOLUTIONARY DEMOCRATIZED ACCESS TO THE DIVINE: this was completely new in human history. Imagine being an Israelite. The only religion you know is Egypt. In Egypt, the priestly class and economic classes are hereditary. Born a slave, die a slave. The priestly class was not available to anyone not born in the right position. And now, this God is telling you that you are all priests to the nations, and sojourners can choose to get circumcised and join you so it’s not just hereditary, and your job is to democratize access to the divine. God is not just for the upper class.   **You who were a slave are to make Yahweh available to all the nations of the world.** (Later Levites within Israel are hereditary, but all Israel is priests to the nations). And this God has commands that you have to follow, but they are moral commands. **He doesn’t depend on you for food, trading you power for his sustenance. He just wants you to be good.**

**Exodus 19:10-15 > Prepare for the mountain, take a bath**

- HOLY MEANS CLEAN: “Holy” means “separate”, but it doesn’t only mean “separate”. A clean person is separate from a person covered in mud, but we would only apply “holy” to the clean person even though the muddy person is also separate.
- FIRST COMMAND AFTER JEWS PROMISE TO FOLLOW HIM: **is to wash your clothes.** You can imagine if you were in God’s position looking down over these 2 million slaves who were chased out of Egypt and wandered through the desert for 3 months with sometimes limited water, just how filthy the entire group would look to you. They left the washing machines back in Egypt. **It makes some sense from that picture that God would say “The first time I appear to a large group of humans, you are going to look presentable.** You will be “holy” from the world around you, cleansed of the dirt and road grime.”
  - DON’T JUST WEAR ANYTHING YOU WANT TO CHURCH: and this verse is why I try to dress differently on Sunday. At church, we come to Sinai to meet with God. And the first thing he wants from us is to look presentable. And you can see how this holiness works today. Imagine walking into a restaurant after church and seeing two families eating: one in their regular clothes, largely unkempt, and one family dressed up, showered, hair combed. **You immediately assume the dressed up family came from church, and the other didn’t.** This is how holiness works. It is a clear signal to others around you that you are a Christian and you take meeting with God seriously.   **You have to be careful not to be self-righteous in your cleanliness, as if you are somehow not just as much a sinner as anyone else, you are the Israelite slave who only cleaned up because God told you to, but you should also recognize that it is net good to do what God wanted of you.**  It is a blessing in your life to gain the dignity of cleanliness and wearing your “Sunday best.”
  - IF YOU HAVE TO CHOOSE: it is better to come to church grungy than not at all. But rarely does this condition persist, where you really have no choice but to come without showering or in your best clothes.
- BAPTISM, DESERT, SANCTIFICATION: Paul told us that the Israelites were baptized into Moses in first Corinthians 10, and I made the point that after the baptism comes the desert, where God tests you. Part of that testing is sanctification. Cleaning you up to get ready to meet God.
  - DO NOT GO NEAR A WOMAN: Leviticus 15:19 later tells us that anyone who has sex must bathe and is unclean until evening. This is in contrast to many pagan gods that were favorites of the people in which sex was part of worship worshiping them. Yahweh says sex is not going to be a part of worshiping him. No sex while you prepare to meet with Yahweh.
  - The text does not record Yahweh telling Moses this directly: Moses has it in his instructions to the people. Probably doesn’t matter, but worth pointing out, especially given the behavior of the people once Moses disappears on the mountain, and they start worshiping their golden calf.
  - There are only actually three things commanded here: wash your clothes, set limits around the mountain so you don’t touch it, and don’t have sex. I’m not sure if anybody could have guessed this in advance. It doesn’t seem somewhat arbitrary of all the things God could have commanded. He could have said “everyone hug their neighbor, give your children some gifts so we’re all in a good mood, sing the song of Moses, then we’ll get started.” Remember, we are reading this to learn about Yahweh because we don’t know things about him. If something seems unexpected, that is a thing that we did not know about him. Cleanliness matters, and sex makes us unclean. Probably not what most of us would like, but that’s what’s here.
  - UNCLEAN VS SINFUL: when we get to Leviticus, we will learn extremely well what exactly it means to be clean and unclean. You just have to take my word for it now that the primary factor of clean and unclean is life and death. Things that represent life are clean, things that represent death are unclean. The primary thing that Christianity has failed to teach us because it’s so often skips the Old Testament, is we think unclean means sinful. It is a fundamental misunderstanding. Sex with marriage is not sinful, but there are things that make you unfit to stand before God for a short period of time. They are sinful in the context of doing them shortly before entering the temple or meeting with God. Like going to the bathroom is not sinful in the bathroom, but if you did it in the middle of the sanctuary, that would be sin . So don’t take away from this that God doesn’t want husbands and wives to have sex, but I understand that if you’re in church and that’s all you’re thinking about, you need to focus better. As Dennis Prager said, baseball managers often ask this of their team Colon don’t have sex the night before the big game so that you are focused with all of your energy on the game. If it’s good enough for baseball, it’s good enough for God to ask before the most momentous event in history since the flood.
- THREE DAYS UNTIL YAHWEH COMES: the last time Yahweh came, an entire nation’s first born died. Three days of anticipation and preparation would build the tension to a climax. He clearly intends to scare them, and that is exactly what happens.
- SHOT OR STONED: they did not have guns, this “shot” is shot with an arrow. Why? Because you can’t run up on the mountain to get him, or you’re touching the mountain now too. So you shoot him from a distance. Man or beast.
- WHY NOT TOUCH THE MOUNTAIN? God is pretty clear, nobody can touch the mountain. Nobody can try to go up. One reason is that anyone who sees God will die, Moses included. But Moses is going up and he survives. So that must not be it.
  - So why capital punishment for this offense? The degree of the punishment is usually an indicator of the degree of the offense. This must be pretty serious.
  - One aspect is just that God said don’t do it, so if you do it in this context, it’s not on accident. You are deliberately disobeying a direct order from God. So that could be part of it.
  - But why would God make this direct order? Why not allow other people to come up the mountain? One answer could be the people are not holy. If God is on the mountain, it is holy ground. To bring sin onto holy ground is an Offense, because the only thing that pays for sin is blood. But Moses goes up on the mountain, and he’s had them sanctifying themselves for 3 days. So the sanctification is enough for them to hear from God, but not enough to go up the mountain. So we still don’t know why they have to clean themselves but they can’t go up.
  - But it struck me this week. There is another more practical reason for this that is right there in the text. Why did God say he was coming in the thick cloud? It was precisely so all the people could see that he spoke through Moses and Moses alone and they should believe Moses. So anybody who tries to go up the mountain is challenging. Moses is authority. They are trying to say “well maybe God can talk through me too, and I can be in charge like Moses.” In fact, this happens later with his sister, Miriam and Aaron and God has to punish them. Miriam’s skin turns leprous and she has to beg Moses to pray to God for her after complaining that she and Aaron should be able to lead to the people too just like Moses.
  - So why would God find it so important to make sure that there is not an oligarchy talking to him, but rather a single mediator? Because Moses points forward to Jesus. After Jesus comes, he is the only way to God. There is one mediator between God and man. You can’t elect yourself mediator. Anyone else who tries to touch the mountain will die. The elders and Aaron can come up later. But first, we have to know Yahweh speaks through his one chosen mediator.
  - Just as God intended to scare the Israelites with his awesome presence so they would beg Moses to be their mediator, so we should fear Yahweh enough that we beg for Jesus to stand before the great and awesome Yahweh so that we do not have to. It would be terrifying to have no mediator. And that is exactly the fate of those who rejected Jesus as their mediator. They think they can touch the mountain themselves and survive.
