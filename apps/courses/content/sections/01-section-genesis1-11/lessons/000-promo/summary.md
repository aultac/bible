

**Summary: Promo**

**Title:** **God creates the universe and man.**

**Storyline Summary:**

- **The Bible itself is a miracle.**
- **We should read the Bible start to finish.**
- **We should load it up in our heads so it feels normal to talk about and use in daily life.**
- **We can read it through a telescope or a microscope.**
- **Both men and women need to load up scripture in their heads.**
- **The Bible is history. It is testable.**
- **History and the Biblical narrative have a shape: a butterfly-like funnel that culminates in Jesus on the cross.**
- **The entire storyline is about God teaching us that we can’t deal with sin without him.**
