# 000-Promo

**First Promo**

- BIBLE ITSELF IS A MIRACLE: It is a single, unified work of history, poetry, prophecy, fulfillment, and theology, written by around 44 different authors over the course of about 2000 years. There is no reasonable rational explanation of why the words on those pages still exist today, are taken seriously by a significant portion of mankind, and have been preserved and attested better than any other event or civilization in history. There is no reason other than the supernatural why this particular story should be the one thing humanity has preserved from history better than any other.
- WE DON’T READ IT UNIFIED: the Bible is one story from start to finish. But we don’t read it that way. We read it as we read no other book: we jump around, reading one sentence here, one sentence there, often out of context, and we don’t put the whole picture together. The Bible covers the entirety of all of history, literally. From the creation of the universe, the beginning of time, all the way to the end of the universe when God destroys it all to create a new one free from sin. It’s very hard to jump into the middle of that story with no background and know what’s really going on.
- WHY LOAD UP THE STORYLINE IN YOUR HEAD? The great Bible teacher from 1980’s made a great point about why we should be carrying the storyline around in our heads. We all see ads for Coca Cola everywhere. All the time. Why? Why does Coke spend so many hundreds of millions of dollars each year getting their name in front of you constantly? Do you not know that Coke exists? Are they trying to make sure you don’t forget Coke exists? No. They are doing that so that **Coke feels normal to you.** Keeping scripture in front of you is what makes it feel normal. It’s what keeps it in your heart. It’s what makes it feel normal to use in conversation. If you’ve ever felt awkward speaking about scripture with someone, it’s because you don’t keep it before your heart often enough, so it feels out of place.
- MICROSCOPE VS. TELESCOPE: you can read the Bible two ways: through a microscope or a telescope. The microscope gets you the detailed meaning of every word, understanding the complete structure of a passage. And it is important to do this because we do not want to misunderstand God’s word. But the other way of reading is through a telescope. And the trouble with only looking through a microscope is that you know that a star is a huge ball of flaming gas as a helium fusion reaction, but you never back up and see a night sky. The Bible is a night sky full of stars, if you’ve never seen them, it’s time to start using that telescope.
- MEN NEED TO LEARN SCRIPTURE: A real man knows what he believes and why he believes it. If you believe the Old Testament, but it never made much sense to you, then you need to man up and figure it out. That is part of the process to turn you into the man God is calling you to be.
- WOMEN NEED TO LEARN SCRIPTURE: Every person in every woman’s life needs to know the words that are in this book, the Bible. They are the words of God given to us for salvation. They are how we encounter Christ. They are how we learn about the Holy Spirit. They are how we learn who Yahweh is and what he wants from us. And everyone you know needs the life that is found only in those pages. And how are you going to give it to the people you care about if you don’t have it loaded up in your head?
- AMOS WELLS POEM READ THE BIBLE THROUGH:
  - I supposed I knew my Bible, Reading piecemeal, hit or miss, Now a bit of John or Matthew, Now a snatch of Genesis, Certain chapters of Isaiah, Certain Psalms (the 23rd), Twelfth of Romans, first of Proverbs Yes, I thought I knew the Word! But I found that thorough reading Was a different thing to do, And the way was unfamiliar When I read the Bible through.
  - You who like to play at Bible, Dip and dabble, here and there, Just before you kneel a-weary, Yawning through a hurried prayer; You who treat the crown of writings As you treat no other book – Just a paragraph disjointed, Just a crude impatient look – Try a worthier procedure, Try a broad and steady view; You will kneel in very rapture When you read the Bible through!

**Second Promo**

- BIBLE IS HISTORY: one difference between Christianity and all other world religions is that our text is history. It is testable. It is not just a book of parables and wise sayings.
- BIBLICAL BUTTERFLY EFFECT: the shape of history and of the Bible looks like a butterfly overall, with a funnel shape.
  - from the creation of the universe encompassing everything,
  - to Earth,
  - to the Middle East,
  - to one man’s family and their promised land with the land surrounding it,
  - to just the southern half of that land after the conquest of northern Israel by the Assyrian empire in 722 BC,
  - to just those people under the rule of others in Babylon and eventually under Rome,
  - to one man’s ministry that starts in a wide area around Galilee through Jerusalem,
  - to his entry into Jerusalem,
  - to an 8-inch by 8-inch hole in the ground in which a beam of wood has been placed.
  - On that cross of wood is a man who, as his blood is dripping into the dirt around that little hole, he splits all of history in two. Every human alive today recons the years of their life to the appearance of that one star in this night sky.
  - Then the narrative widens back out to Jerusalem,
  - to Asia Minor in Acts,
  - to the rest of the world of Christendom,
  - until we get to Revelation at the end encompassing the entire universe again.
- JESUS WAS GOD: Jesus was the middle-aged son of a somewhat unremarkable carpenter from a backwater town in the middle of of nowhere Israel. He was poor and had no major accomplishments or authority, but around age 30 he starts up an itinerate ministry, gathers a few supporters around him, seemed to do miracles, and he claimed to be God and gets killed for it by the authorities 3 years later. There is no reason we should ever have heard of this guy, much less have his life, death, and reported resurrection as the central point on which all of human history has been split, unless he actually WAS God.
- THE STORY IS ABOUT DEALING WITH SIN: Man begins living with God, God ends it with God living with man. And what happens in the middle is sin. And the story of the Bible is dealing with sin. And it’s not because God didn’t know how to deal with sin, it’s because we don’t.
  - First, there is only one rule: don’t eat the fruit. Easy enough. First man and woman broke it.
  - Conscience came next: when we ate the fruit we got a little voice in our heads telling us right from wrong. Doesn’t work: the first brother murders the second brother.
  - Good genetic stock came next. It’s not long on just the conscience that all the thoughts of all mankind are all evil all day long. So God picks the best guy alive at the time, Noah and his family, and wipes out everyone else with a flood. All humans will now be descended from the genes of the guy who was the best in his generation. Not long after that he is passed out drunk and naked on the bed and his son Ham and grandson Canaan defile him in some way. Didn’t work.
  - Nations come next: then at Babel humanity gathers together to build a tower to unite heaven and earth to be their own gods. So God says, “Fine, if you don’t want me to be your god, I’ll give you gods, and they won’t be me.” So he creates all the nations and assigns one to each of his sons, the sons of God. **But God says**, “**I am going to be my own son: I will keep one nation as an inheritance for myself**.” This doesn’t work: the nations try to kill his people for all of history, and his people don’t generally want the job, they constantly end up worshipping other gods.
  - Miracles come next: God does great miracles for his people and that doesn’t work. They saw miracles every day and all they did was get tired of the food falling from the sky.
  - God comes to live on Earth with man: God tells us something we didn’t know about him: he won’t be where sin is. The physical ground soaks up sin. Anywhere God is going to be requires the sin that is soaked into the ground to be cleansed before God will sit there. So he gives the people instructions on how to build such a place, the Holy of Holies in the Tabernacle. And the only thing that can cleanse it is blood. The only thing that pays for sin is death. Sin is so serious that something has to die. And God allows an innocent animal to die in the place of the sinful humans, and it’s blood is used constantly to cleanse the tent so that the pillar of cloud and fire can sit right there in the tent in the midst of the Hebrews. They could talk straight to God, ask him questions, and get answers. And this doesn’t work either, they worship other gods for the rest of the story.
  - God comes as savior and helper: By the time of Ezekiel, the pillar of cloud and fire has moved into the temple that Solomon built, but it gives up on them and picks up and floats out over the mountains, never to return to that place until hundreds of years later when God, who is both son and father, rides into Jerusalem and overturns the tables of the money changers in the temple. And through the spilling of his perfect blood, all our bodies have become temples in which the Holy Spirit can now reside, creating holy ground wherever a Christian walks. It turns out the only thing that can take care of sin is God. And it is through our gratitude to him for saving us from the slavery of sin that we can finally try to stop sinning and do what God wants.
