- MORE EGYPTIAN GODS
  - Sympathize with Moses: he had to sit in class as a young child in the palace learning all the details of all the Egyptian gods so that he was well-versed in their entire theology. **We only have to learn a few.**
  - HATHOR: Hathor is one side of a schizophrenic goddess (Sekhmet is her dark side). Dr. Jekyll and Mrs. Hyde. Hathor is a cow-headed goddess of the “good” feminine qualities: motherhood, love, beauty. Daughter of Ra and wife of Horus. One of the oldest and most revered gods of all Egypt.
  - SEKHMET: Hathor’s dark side, **the darkside of the feminine**, the irrationally violent “momma bear.” Lion-headed Goddess of plague, war, destruction. The story goes that Ra was getting old and weak and humanity figured this out, so they planned a rebellion against Ra. Ra decides to send his “enforcer”: the dark side of his daughter Hathor. She transforms fully into the lion-headed goddess Sekhmet and is so cruel and violent that she nearly wipes out all of humanity. Ra has regrets, and stops Sekhment by tricking her into getting drunk on beer dyed to look like blood. She thought she was drinking blood, but becomes drunk and reverts back to gentle Hathor.
  - PTAH/APIS: Apis is bull god of strength and fertility syncretized as the living embodiment of Ptah, the Memphis tradition’s creator-god who **conceived the entire universe in his mind and then spoke it into existence**. In this respect, that he supposedly created the universe by speaking, he is one of the most Yahweh-like gods in the Egyptian pantheon. Ptah is the god of craftsmen as the original craftsman. The Apis bull is always a bull with a white spot on its head thought to be conceived by lightning. On death, Apis bull is embalmed and buried in Serapeum that we covered a couple weeks ago.
    - POLYTHEIM AND MOST HIGH GODS: For some reason, polytheistic religions always have this “overall” creator that sounds a lot like Yahweh, but isn’t quite Yahweh. **It seems to be the demon’s favorite pastime to convince people there is a “most high god”, and they are that most high god instead of the actual Most High God.** They usually even have multiple, conflicting “most high gods”, like Hinduism has Brahma, Vishnu, Shiva and Shakti, all rival conceptions of “most high god” that various traditions explain away with their own syncretism or mythologies.
    - GIBRIL: Just like the demon Gibril who appeared to Muhammed and tortured him before his cousins convinced him it was the angel Gabriel, who then gave him the Quran and convinced him that Allah was the most high god. A god who muslims imagine is the same as Yahweh, but whose revealed actions in the world show him to be Satan himself. Recall the “come and see” point the we talked about last week where God shows us his qualities through his actions. **The “come and see” of Allah showed a god of forced conversion (the opposite of the God of the 10 plagues who strengthened Pharoah’s heart to give him his free will), mass slavery, mass violent rape and kidnapping of women and girls, beating women into submission, and institutionalized lying to achieve jihad.**  This is not the God of the 10 plagues, this is his enemy.
- WHY NOT JUST APPEAR TO PHARAOH DIRECTLY? Why does God use Moses and tell Moses to say all these things and do these things before pharaoh? Why doesn’t God just appear to pharaoh directly and say I am Yahweh? We don’t really know why God did it this way and not some other way. **But we do know that this way was so spectacular that it has been told ever since that time.** “So that my name might be proclaimed throughout all the nations.” Because he did the plagues this way, Pharoah, the Egyptians, the Hebrews, Moses, and now you also know now that Yahweh is “He Is.” You can’t argue with the effectiveness of the approach through Moses.
- NATURAL VERSUS MIRACULOUS EXPLANATIONS: We finished last week with the Nile-into-blood plague. There are many people who seem to have a bias against the supernatural, who analyze these stories in Exodus and try to find naturalistic explanations for them.
  - SNAKES WERE JUST STIFF: For example, I had heard before that the magicians were able to charm a snake to make the snake stiff like a staff, and that’s what they did when they turned their staff into snakes. It was already a snake, it was just stiff.
  - RED MUD/ALGAE ILLUSION: I have heard people claim that the red blood in the Nile was actually red mud from a mountain way upstream or a red algae bloom that made the water toxic to fish. **They are convinced the magicians of pharaoh are tricksters**, fooling Moses and pharaoh just like an illusionist of today. Does Pharoah sound like the kind of guy who would be fooled by tricksters? Is God also a trickster?
  - BIBLE IS CLEAR: I do not understand why Christians are so often blind to the fact that their attraction to naturalistic explanations is entirely a bias from our secular, non-super-natural culture. **The text itself clearly says their staff turned into snakes. It clearly says the Nile turned into blood, not that it turned red like blood.** I**t clearly says the Egyptian priests did the same thing God did with the frogs through their own secret arts.** This entire book is describing repeated supernatural intervention in the natural world. If you believe this book is true, then take it at its Word.
  - GOD CAN DO ANYTHING: When it says a miracle happened, it happened. Philosophically, there’s absolutely no problem with the creator of the entire universe being able to do the kinds of things described here.
  - BIBLE NEVER HINTS AT FAKE MAGICIANS: There is no hint in the text that the magicians are fake. On the contrary, the text seems to suggest that their spells actually do work. Their occult rituals, given to them by their gods actually do what they say they do.
  - FAKE ILLUSIONS ARE EASIER FOR BUGS: If they were fake, it would have been just as easy to turn a ball of dirt into a bowl of gnats as it was to turn water into blood, or to make frogs appear. What is so striking about the magician’s response to the gnats is that they are completely baffled. **All of their massive books containing all of their spells had no known means of turning dust into those insects.**  This is just like 400 years ago when the magicians could not interpret Pharoah’s dream. Their books had no means to achieve the real outcome. In their 2000 year history that gave them this incredibly complex set of rituals and theology, nothing like this had ever been revealed to them by their gods, and their current prayers to their gods about it went unanswered.
- GOD INTRODUCES HIMSELF TO HUMANITY THROUGH PLAGUES: God has not revealed himself to any large group of people prior to this. Here, he reveals himself to humanity first with awful plagues: blood, frogs, gnats, flies, disease, boils, hail. **God’s first explicit interaction with large-scale humanity, at least post-flood, is plagues as punishment for evil.** We will revisit this topic in depth after we get through the plagues to discuss why God chose plagues as his first revelation to humanity.

**Exodus 8:1-7 > Plague 2: Frogs**

- GODS RELATING TO PLAGUE 2:
  - Heket. Fertility, wife of Khnum, midwife to humanity, who women prayed to in childbirth. Khnum fashioned humanity out of clay on his potter’s wheel, and Heket breathed life into the clay. One might ask what Khnum saw in Heket. She has head of a frog. Apparently she had a really nice personality… Frogs would appear and precede the annual flooding every year, it was a sign of new life.
- GODDESS OF THE MIDWIVES: Heket was the goddess of midwives and childbirth. This is also fitting for one of the first plagues against the baby killers.   **The frog-headed goddess of childbirth cannot stop the frogs from attacking the Egyptian baby killers**.
- OCCULT COMEDY: I feel like the scene with the magicians must’ve been quite hilarious. They went before pharaoh. They had worked out all the spells. They had gotten the extra frogs and they went up to pharaoh and they’re like “See more frogs!” Then pharaoh calls them up close to him, he says “Come here.” Then he whacks them over the head with his staff repeatedly while he says “We already went over this! I don’t need more frogs, I need less frogs! Why in the world would you figure out a spell to make more frogs when we already have too many! First, you make more blood when we already have an entire river full of blood, and now you make more frogs when our whole nation is already crawling with them! How about you go figure out how to do something useful? **I’m surrounded by idiots**!”

**Exodus 8:8-15 > Plead with Yahweh, you can go**

- MOSES SAYS PICK A CARD: much like a magician today tells the audience member to pick a card so the trick is more believable, Moses tells Pharaoh he can pick the time. And pharaoh, in his stubbornness, instead of saying stop the frogs right now, he doesn’t want to give Moses the satisfaction of possibly having planned the whole thing, so he unexpectedly picks tomorrow. **I am sure all of the courtiers around him were inwardly shaking their heads when he said “tomorrow.”**
- MOSES CRIED TO YAHWEH: the Hebrew word is more like screamed and cried, it signifies a great wailing. This differs from the rest of the times where it just said Moses prayed to God. He cries to God. The commentators suggest this means Moses is really bothered by the innocent Egyptians getting hurt right along with the guilty ones. Moses has personal angst over their suffering and cries to God for it to stop. He doesn’t cry tomorrow, he cries now right after meeting with Pharoah. Dennis Prager points out, Moses himself grew up Egyptian.   **It must have pained him to see his culture until he was 40 hurting like this.**
- WHO HARDENED? Who hardened Pharoah’s heart after plagues 1 and 2? It just “became hard” or it was Pharaoh himself who did it. Not Yahweh yet.

**Exodus 8:16-19 > Plague 3: Gnats**

- UNCLEAR WORD: the word here for Gnats only appears in this one place in the Bible. “Kinnim” is apparently literally “little bugs” . So it’s very hard to know exactly what it means. Some translate it gnats, some lice. Some kind of tiny annoying insect.
- GODS RELATING TO PLAGUE 3:
  - Geb. Dust into Gnats. Geb is God of earth. Married to the his twin sister, goddess of the sky, Nut. They were initially joined so tightly there was no space between earth and sky, so the Sun god Ra commanded their dad, Shu, to separate them. Nut (sky) was raised up in an arch above Geb (earth), creating cosmic order and space for humans. Geb and Nut fathered Osiris, Isis, and their siblings.
- NO WARNING: end of the first triad, this plague had no warning.
- WHO HARDENED: Pharoah’s heart “was hardened”, not by Yahweh.

**Exodus 8:20-24 > Plague 4: Flies or Scarabs**

- UNCLEAR WORD: the text actually just says the word for swarms. **It does not say what the swarms will be of.** It could be flies, it could be beetles. Some kind of flying insect larger than the ones formed from the dust.
- SCARABS WERE SACRED: there is some reason to think it could have been scarab beetles given their prominence in Egyptian religion through Khepri and elsewhere.
- GODS RELATING TO PLAGUE 4
  - Shu: primordial god of atmosphere, separates Nut from Geb. Swarms filled the air, Shu couldn’t stop it.
  - Khepri: scarab-headed god. God of creation, renewal, and rebirth in afterlife. Rolls the sun across the sky each day like a scarab dung beetle rolls the ball of dung from which its eggs hatch. Associated with Ra the sun god.
- WHY DID GOD SET APART GOSHEN? Did he set apart Goshen because the Hebrews were not sinners like the Egyptians and they didn’t deserve the plagues that hit the Egyptians? The God who normally sends rain on the just and the unjust alike? **He doesn’t say he’s setting apart the Hebrews because of anything they did or anything they suffered at the hands of the Egyptians.**  He says he’s setting them apart because he has chosen them to be his people, in other words only because of something Yahweh did. And he is doing it for the purpose of teaching Pharaoh that he is Yahweh “in the midst of the earth.”

**Exodus 8:25-end > Plead with Yahweh, go sacrifice but stay close**

- YAHWEH COULD BE WORKING WITH OTHER GODS: prior to this, Pharoah could have been thinking that Yahweh was making deals with Egyptian gods: he gets to plague the Egyptians if they get to do the same plague on the Hebrews since the Hebrews experienced the first triad of plagues. But now, it’s no question, Yahweh is in charge.
- PHAROAH LEARNED GOD IS NOT LIMITED IN TERRITORY: a point from Nachum Sarna, when Yahweh set apart Goshen in this plague he showed Pharoah that he is not limited to his own territory like all other gods. **He showed him that Yahweh is in the very midst of Egypt.** So Pharoah’s genius mind quickly determines well if Yahweh is here, why do I need to let you go out to the wilderness? You can just sacrifice to Yahweh here! Problem solved, still my slaves.
- PHARAOH KNOWS THEY INTEND TO LEAVE FOR GOOD: he finally understands they aren’t coming back after they worship their God. **And now he is completely reevaluating his relationship with the Hebrews.**  Remember the original reason, the Hebrews were enslaved by his predecessor was to break their spirit so they would not join with the enemies of Egypt in a war. **If that were still the case, he would be more than happy for them to just leave.**  But pharaoh has done some soul-searching, and now he realizes that their purpose in Egypt is not just to be his toys that he can torture however he wants in the name of saving Egypt from invaders. Their purpose is no longer solely to have a broken spirit. He sees the complete loss of a 1M+ workforce now, and he has decided he doesn’t want to let them go. **He is like a young child who tortures a feral cat and then is sad when it runs away.**
- NOW MOSES IS PLAYING CHESS: Moses is very quick here. He has the perfect answer to pharaoh’s negotiation. And now pharaoh has no clever response to give back to him, he just shuts up. **Strangely, so does not even object to the idea that they would be sacrificing the sacred animals of Egypt.** He just concedes the point.
- PHARAOH HAS TO BEG: the tables have now turned to the point that Pharaoh knows he has to beg Moses to plead with Yahweh on his behalf

**9:1-7 > Plague 5: Livestock**

- GOD SPECIFIES TIME: this is the first plague when God had Moses tell pharaoh a specific time that it would occur. This is like spanking your kids in the moment vs. scheduling a later time to spank them. It’s WAY worse for the kids if they have to anticipate it for a couple of hours. Pharoah was probably up all night tossing and turning with dread.
- NOT ALL LIVESTOCK: Only Egyptian livestock “in the field”. **Not the livestock of slaves or other non-Egyptians or livestock in barns or stables.**
- PHAROAH DOESN’T BELIEVE IT: he has to send someone to check!
- GODS RELATING TO PLAGUE 5:
  - Hathor: female cow. Goddess of fertility and motherhood.
  - Apis: sacred bull of Memphis, incarnation of creator-god Ptah. God of strength and fertility. Not strong enough to fight Yahweh!

**9:8-12 > Plague 6: Boils**

- NO WARNING ON THIRD PLAGUES: ends the second triad.
- MAGICIANS DISGRACED: they went from replicating Moses to unable to even stand before him. There was no trying to replicate the boils via.an occult spell. These are the most intellectual, elevated people in their society. These are their high priests.
- YAHWEH HARDENED THIS TIME: **the first five plagues Pharoah hardened his own heart**, or it just “became hard” on its own. Starting at plagues six God hardens his heart.
- HARDENED IS STRENGTHENED: the Hebrew word translated this time as “hardened” (chazaq) is actually “strengthened” which is much clearer about what is going on. Pharoah hardened his own heart the first 5 times, he deserves it now.
