

**Summary: Exodus 8-9:12**

**Title:** **Frogs and gnats plague Egyptians and Hebrews alike but then the flies, death of livestock in the fields and terrible boils attack only the Egyptians. The Egyptian priests and their gods quickly fall short of power and pharaoh seems to relent, but instead his heart is hardened.**

**Storyline Summary:**

- **Frogs, frogs, and more frogs**
- **Magicians did the same (again...)**
- **Pharoah begs Moses to take away the frogs**
- **Moses cries in great distress to take away the frogs**
- **God takes away the frogs, but their bodies remain**
- **Pharoah’s heart hardened**
- **Gnats/Lice**
- **Magicians could not replicate and say this is the finger of Elohim**
- **Flies/Scarabs: they don’t affect Goshen**
- **Pharoah says if Yahweh is here, just worship him here**
- **Moses responds Egypt would stone us (because these are their gods)**
- **Livestock plague: livestock “in the field” died**
- **Pharoah lied, pharoah lied, ...**
- **Boils**
