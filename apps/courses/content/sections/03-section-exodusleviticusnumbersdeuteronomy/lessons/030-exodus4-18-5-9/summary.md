

**Summary: Exodus 4:18-5:9**

**Title:** **Moses returns to Egypt with Aaron, convinces the Hebrews with the signs, and fails at their first meeting with Pharoah.**

**Storyline Summary:**

- **Moses asks Jethro for approval to go back to Egypt**
- **Donkey express to Egypt with the family**
- **God instructed Moses again on what to say**
- **God tells Moses he will harden Pharoah’s heart**
- **Field Surgery creates Bridegroom of Blood**
- **Moses and Aaron are reunited**
- **Moses and Aaron speak to the elders and the Hebrews**
- **They went to see Pharoah and butchered God’s instructions**
- **Pharaoh says “Who is this Yahweh” and orders no straw be given for brickmaking.**
