

**Summary: Exodus 6:10-7**

**Title:** **Moses and Aaron go before Pharaoh with the sign of their staff turning into a serpent, the magicians do the same and Pharaoh’s heart is hardened. Next the water of the land is turned to blood, the magicians once again replicate and Pharaoh’s heart is hardened even further.**

**Storyline Summary:**

- **God tells Moses to go to Pharoah**
- **Moses says “the Hebrews didn’t even listen to me, how will Pharoah?”**
- **Moses has “Uncircumsized lips”**
- **Genealogy of Moses and Aaron**
- **Moses still has uncircumsized lips**
- **God instructs Moses and Aaron for Round 2**
- **Staff turns into serpent, Aaron’s staff swallows the priests’ staffs**
- **Pharoah’s heart is hardened**
- **Water in the Nile turned to blood (and in the pots)**
- **Priests do the same**
- **Pharoah’s heart remained hardened.**
