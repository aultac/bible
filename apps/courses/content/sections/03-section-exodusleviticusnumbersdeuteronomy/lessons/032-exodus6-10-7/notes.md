**Exodus 6:10-13 > Go back to Pharaoh, Moses is unsure of himself**

- UNCIRCUMSIZED LIPS: this is an odd phrase. It can mean three things:
  - SOMETHING IN THE WAY: First, that “something is in the way” like a foreskin that prevent the words from his words mouth from being effective, as the Bible describes “uncircumcised ears” elsewhere meaning something is in the way of hearing or an “uncircumcised heart” means something is in the way of turning to God.
  - GOD’S COMMAND: Second, circumcision is the symbol of accepting the promise of God to Abraham. “Uncircumcised” refers to being outside that promise, like the Gentiles. It means not perfectly following the commands of God. Abraham knows this well after the “bridegroom of blood” incident. He keeps saying he doesn’t want to do what God commands.
  - UNHOLY AND SINFUL: Third, it can mean “unclean” or “unholy”, similar to when Isaiah had a vision and stood before God and the first thing he did was express an intense feeling of dirtiness and sin before the Holy God: Isaiah 6:5 “Woe is me! For I am lost; for I am a man of unclean lips, and I dwell in the midst of a people of unclean lips; for my eyes have seen the King, the LORD of hosts!””

**Exodus 7:1-13 > Two old men go to Pharoah for Staff Into Snake miracle**

- JANNES AND JAMBRES: 2 Timothy 3:8 tells us that the names of the two primary magicians were Jannes and Jambres. It was not recorded in the Old Testament, but it appears to exist in extra biblical literature and then used by Paul his letter to Timothy.
- TWO OLD MEN: imagine two 80-year-old brothers walking in to 27-year-old Pharoah, stooped, holding onto a staff. They already lost to Pharaoh once. Pharaoh is probably surprised they were willing to show their face again. **But this time, Pharaoh Machiavelli did not know how to answer them because God got involved.**
- SNAKE IS SYMBOL OF PHAROAH’S POWER: Remember the “uraeus" (Cobra) is the symbol of Pharoah’s power through the god Widget. So this scene has Pharoah’s priests create nachash (“shining ones”, snakes), but Yahweh’s power is greater than Pharoah’s power and Yahweh’s snake eats Pharoah’s snake. This is literally like beating someone at their own game or on their home turf. Yahweh’s power doesn’t derive from the symbol of a snake, but Pharoah’s does, and **Yahweh adopts Pharoah’s own symbol and one-ups him with it.**
- FUN FACT ABOUT TANNIN: Aaron’s staff this time actually becomes a “tannin”. It is the same word used for what God created on day 5. The Septuagint translated it as “whales”, so our later translations turned it into “great sea creatures.” The Greeks translated it as “whale” because that was the biggest thing they knew of since “tannin” has a concept of “big and terrible.” **But nobody thinks Moses’s staff turned into a whale before Pharoah.** However, we do think it turned into a particularly terrible reptile that ate the other snakes, i.e. the same “family” of creature as the snake. Do you know what the Greek word is for great and terrible reptile? Dino - great/terrible, saurus - reptile: “Dinosaurus”. The Hebrew text literally should be translated into Greek that on day 5 God created the Dinosaurs in the sea and the birds. But the Septuagint translators didn’t know about dinosaurs yet, so they went with giant sea creature, i.e. whale.

**Introduction To Plagues:**

- TRIADIC STRUCTURE:
  - 1, 4, 7: meet Pharoah in morning where he is
  - 2, 5, 8: go to palace
  - 3, 6, 9: no warning
  - First 3 plagues: **environment** (nile, land, dust)
  - Second 3 plagues: **animals and humans**  (flies/scarabs, livestock, boils)
  - Third 3 plagues: **cosmic heavens and earth** (hail, complete locust destruction, darkness)
- INTRO TO EGYPTIAN GODS: the plagues attack Egyptian gods. So we need to learn about some of them to fully understand the plagues.
  - There were a lot of Egyptian gods in their pantheon. The stories of the gods were also changing a lot over hundreds of years. There were also regional variations. They also really liked “syncretism”: combining gods or religions together into harmonized versions. Kind of like polysemy for gods. Studying Egyptian gods feels almost identical to studying Hindu gods today. I’ve attempted to put together a coherent introduction to the most important gods, especially those related to the plagues.
  - **Ra**: Sun god, emerges from chaos, self-created. King of all the gods. Merged with “Amun” as the hidden mysterious “unknowable” god to be Amun-Ra at the time of the Exodus, the supreme, but not fully knowable, god.
  - Ra creates **Shu** (air) and **Tefnut** (rain/dew)
  - Shu and Tefnut have children **Geb** (Earth) and **Nut** (sky/heavens)
  - **SHOW THE PICTURE**: Geb + Nut are tightly embraced, their dad Shu separates them into Earth and Sky. Shu is “air” between.
  - Geb + Nut have children **Isis** (magic/healing), **Osiris** (fertility, agriculture, afterlife), **Set** (storms, chaos), also Nepthys.
  - OSIRIS MYTH:
    - Osiris is benevolent king over Egypt. Brings agriculture, law, and prosperity. **Originally god of agriculture.**
    - Set gets jealous of successful older brother Osiris, kills him, throws his coffin in the Nile. Coffin washes on shore and a tamarisk tree grows around it. His wife/sister Isis eventually finds the coffin and hides the body. Set finds the body again and dismembers the body and throws the 14 pieces into the Nile and they are lost all across Egypt.
    - Isis finds Osiris body parts and reassembles him. Can’t find one part: the “part” needed so she can have a child with him. She fashions one out of gold or wood depending on the version of the story, and then uses her magic to temporarily resurrect him to have child Horus. Once Horus is conceived, Osiris has to leave this world for the realm of the dead (can’t stay alive), and his dad Geb gives him the afterlife/underworld to rule.
    - Egyptians all aspired to live moral lives in the hope that they too would be resurrected like Osiris and live in “paradise” (the Field of Reeds) in the afterlife. **To earn their way to heaven and resurrection.**
    - **The Nile is considered Osiris’ bloodstream.**  To attack the Nile means attacking the god they all hoped to become, jeopardizing their chance at “heaven”.
  - Isis + Osiris have **Horus** after Osiris resurrection. Horus battles Set to become rightful king of Egypt and eventually wins. All pharaohs are descendants of Horus, and **the “current” Pharoah is always considered to be the actual incarnation of Horus.**
  - Horus’s right eye is the sun and his left eye is the moon. In a battle with Set, Set gouges out his right eye. Isis heals his missing eye and the “Eye of Horus” becomes a symbol of protection and healing. Because his right eye is the sun, he is syncretized with Ra as **Ra-Horakhty** and comes to also represent the full path of the sun from horizon to horizon.
  - **Khnum**: creator of humanity, god of the source of the Nile. He created humanity on his potter’s wheel from Nile clay. No clear origin story, seems to be self-created from Nun like Ra, but below Ra in importance.
  - **Heket**: frog-headed wife of Khnum, goddess of the spring floods. Breathed life into the clay and became goddess of motherhood, childbirth, and midwives. No preserved creation story available for Heket, possibly a daughter of Ra but inconclusive.
  - **Hapi**: god of abundance who controls the yearly Nile flooding that brought life to Egypt. No preserved origin story.
  - **Renenutet**: goddess of protecting the harvest, Cobra goddess. Origin story is not preserved, but she protected the harvest each year before, during, and after harvest in storage. Relevant because all the vegetation and grain is decimated by the plagues.
  - **Khepri**: scarab beetle god of rebirth and sun. Manifestation of Ra. **Rolls the sun across the sky each day** as a dung beetle scarab rolls the ball of dung across the desert. Scarab beetles lay eggs in the dung balls and they seem to just “magically” appear as life from the dung ball. So he represents creation of new life and the rebirth of the sun rolling across the sky each day.
  - Missing: Hathor+Sekhmet, Apis

**Exodus 7:14-end > Plague 1: Nile Into Blood**

- FITTING FIRST PLAGUE: this all began with the previous pharaoh, throwing the male Hebrew children into the Nile, shedding their blood. So much blood is shed that it is fitting the entire river turns to blood.
- GODS RELATING TO PLAGUE 1:
  - Khnum: created humanity w/ wife Heket, he is source of Nile
  - Hapi: specifically god of the spring floods that make Egypt prosperous
  - Osiris: Nile is his bloodstream, he is what all Egyptians aspired to.
- FRACTAL BLOOD: everything that drank the blood of Osiris, the Egyptian dying and rising God, dies. The blood of Osiris brings death, not resurrection, not life. That 1400 years in the future, the true dying and rising God will spill his blood on the Earth, and everyone who drinks of his blood will live forever. When we take communion and drink of the living water, turns to blood, we get the opposite of what the Egyptians got drinking the blood of their dying and rising god. The blood of Osiris stains everything it touches. But the blood of Jesus cleanses, making things whiter than the whitest bleached linens.
- WHY NOT JUST APPEAR TO PHARAOH DIRECTLY? Why does God use Moses and tell Moses to say all these things and do these things before pharaoh? Why doesn’t God just appear to pharaoh directly and say I am Yahweh? We don’t really know why God did it this way and not some other way. But we do know that this way was so spectacular that it has been told ever since that time. Because he did the plagues this way, Pharoah, the Egyptians, the Hebrews, Moses, and now you also know now that Yahweh is “He Is.” You can’t argue with the effectiveness of the approach.
- NATURAL VERSUS MIRACULOUS EXPLANATIONS: there are many people who seem to have a bias against the supernatural, who analyze these stories in Exodus and try to find naturalistic explanations for them.
  - SNAKES WERE JUST STIFF: For example, I had heard before that the magicians were able to charm a snake to make the snake stiff like a staff, and that’s what they did when they turned their staff into snakes. It was already a snake, it was just stiff.
  - RED MUD/ALGAE ILLUSION: I have heard people claim that the red blood in the Nile was actually red mud from a mountain way upstream or a red algae bloom that made the water toxic to fish. They are convinced the magicians of pharaoh are tricksters, fooling Moses and pharaoh just like an illusionist of today.
  - BIBLE IS CLEAR: I do not understand why this is so attractive to people. The text itself clearly says their staff turned into snakes. It clearly says the Nile turned into blood, not that it turned red like blood. It clearly says they did the same thing God did with the frogs through their own secret arts. This entire book is describing constant supernatural intervention in the natural world. If you believe this book is true, then take it at its Word.
  - GOD CAN DO ANYTHING: When it says a miracle happened, it happened. Philosophically, there’s absolutely no problem with the creator of the entire universe being able to do the kinds of things described here.
  - BIBLE NEVER HINTS AT FAKE MAGICIANS: There is no hint in the text that the magicians are fake. On the contrary, the text seems to suggest that their spells actually do work. Their occult rituals, given to them by their gods actually do what they say they do.
  - FAKE ILLUSIONS ARE EASIER FOR BUGS: If they were fake, it would have been just as easy to turn a ball of dirt into a bowl of gnats as it was to turn water into blood, or to make frogs appear. What is so striking about the magician’s response to the gnats is that they are completely baffled. All of their massive books containing all of their spells had no known means of turning dust into those insects. This is just like 400 years ago when the magicians could not interpret Pharoah’s dream. Their books had no means to achieve the real outcome.
  - They didn’t say this is the finger of Elohim just because they couldn’t put a sheet over a bowl of dirt and swap it out for a bowl of insects. They said that because in their 2000 year history that gave them this incredibly complex set of rituals and theology, nothing like this had ever been revealed to them by their gods, and their current prayers to their gods about it went unanswered.
- OCCULT COMEDY: I just want you to picture for a minute the priests (“magicians”).
  - PRIESTS REMEMBER A SPELL: They see the Nile turn of blood and they start getting excited. One turns to the other and says I was just reading a spell about this the other day. We can do this!
  - PHAROAH GAINS CONFIDENCE: Pharaoh sees their obvious excitement, and after getting over his shock of finding no clean water in any of his jars or in the River, the magicians tell him “we got this!” so they disappear for a little bit where they’re trying to figure out how can we get some clean water to prove we can do this?
  - PRIESTS DIG FOR CLEAN WATER: They start digging beside the Nile, and they find some clean water and they put it in a bowl.
  - PHAROAH MISTAKES CLEAN WATER FOR SUCCESS: They take it up to pharaoh and he sees the clean water in the bowl and he looks at Moses and says “HA! My magicians are coming to take care of you.”
  - PRIESTS RUSH TO MAKE BLOOD: Before he can say anything to them, they run right up and start waving their hands over the bowl. The water in the bowl turns to blood, and they start jumping up and down, excited pointing at Moses “see we can do the same thing. Your God is not so great!”
  - PHAROAH WHISPERS TO PRIESTS: the smile slowly falls off of pharaoh‘s face. And he looked at the blood in the bowl, and he looked at the magicians, and he looked back at the blood. Not wanting to embarrass himself in front of Moses, he leans over to the excited, magician and whispers in his ear. I thought you had turned the blood back into water. You mean to tell me you found clean water in the first thing you did was turn it into more blood?”
  - PHAROAH PRETENDS HE KNEW ALL ALONG: then he turns back to Moses, recovering himself, and says we are just as powerful as you!” And then he went back into his house, surrounded by blood.
