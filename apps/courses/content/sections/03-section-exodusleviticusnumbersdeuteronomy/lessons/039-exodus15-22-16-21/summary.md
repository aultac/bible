

**Summary: Exodus 15:22-16:21**

**Title:** **The Israelites continue to grumble in the desert after the Red Sea, and Yahweh continues to provide, giving them water, quail and manna, with clear instructions on how to receive His gifts and rely on him each day so they will once again know that He is Yahweh their God.**

**Storyline Summary:**

- **Bitter water is made sweet by throwing in a log, just like the Cross**
- **God gave Israel a statute to listen to him and obey and they won’t get the diseases of Egypt**
- **Elim comes after Marah**
- **The grumbling continues**
- **Yahweh gave them manna and quail to eat with commands for the manna**
