

**Summary: Exodus 2:16-3:12**

**Title:** **Moses saves Reuel’s daughters and gets a wife from among them. God hears Israel's cries and calls Moses out of a burning bush that he will be used to bring the children of Israel out of Egypt.**

**Storyline Summary:**

- **Moses saves the daughters of Reuel/Jethro**
- **Moses marries Zipporah**
- **Moses and Zipporah have a son, Gershom**
- **King of Egypt died**
- **God hears the cry of his people**
- **Burning Bush**
- **God warns Moses that he is standing on Holy Ground, take off your sandals**
- **God identifies himself as the God of his ancestors**
- **God tells Moses he’s the guy to take his people out of Egypt**
