

**Summary: Exodus 16:22-17**

**Title:** **The Israelites gather a double portion of manna on the 6th day and a jar of manna was kept for future generations. They complain of thirst and Moses strikes the rock and Yahweh gives them water. Then Israel defeats Amalek with Yahweh’s help by Moses holding the staff of God over his head during the battle.**

**Storyline Summary:**

- **Gather manna six days a week but not on the seventh, gather extra on the sixth: the first Sabbath**
- **Some still did not do what God said: they went out on the seventh day to gather.**
- **God explains the purpose of the Sabbath: I gave it to you as a day of rest**
- **Omer is a tenth of an ephah!!**
- **Save some manna in a jar**
- **People ran out of water, and they say it’s Moses’ fault**
- **Moses got hung up on the grumbling**
- **Moses strikes the rock, we get 100 semi loads of water a day**
- **Amalekites attack the stragglers**
- **Moses holds up his staff and Israel wins**
- **General Joshua shows up on the scene to defeat Amalek**
