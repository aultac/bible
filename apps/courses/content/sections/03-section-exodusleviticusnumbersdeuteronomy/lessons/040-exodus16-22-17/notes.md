# 040_Exodus16:22-17

- MANNA MEANS WHAT? No, he’s on second. I Don’t Know’s on third. Manna literally means “what is it”. A lot of Abbot and Costello conversations were likely being had in the desert that day. Son: “Ma, you want me to go get what?” Mom: “Yes”. Son: “But what am I supposed to get?” Mom: “Yes, you are supposed to get what.”
- REVIEW: MAN DOES NOT LIVE BY BREAD ALONE: The advantage of how Moses laid out the Torah is we get Deuteronomy (“retelling”) to help explain things like this. **Read Deuteronomy 8:1-10.** The purpose of the Manna was to teach Israel and everyone who came after that man does not live by bread alone but by every word that comes from the mouth of Yahweh. The original purpose was not primarily to feed them, it was to teach them this. Jesus uses this to refute Satan when he is hungry and Satan tells him to turn stones into bread so he can eat.
- WHY DOES MANNA ROT IF YOU STORE IT? What is God trying to teach us here? The obvious first reading is they are going to need to depend on God every day. God did that intentionally. But we know from the New Testament that Jesus is the bread of life sent down from heaven, he is a fractal with the manna.
  - HOARDING MANNA MEANT DISTRUSTING GOD: When the Israelites used the bread from heaven against God’s explicit instruction, to hoard it up for themselves, so they wouldn’t have to trust God in the future, **the blessing from heaven became a plague.** The blessing that fell from heaven becomes a plague that fell from heaven, just as plagues fell from heaven on the Egyptians. Delicious food became bugs, decay, and stench.
  - I REFUSE TO BELIEVE IN A GOD WHO WOULD DO THAT: it came to me this week that impossible conversation that might have occurred when Moses saw the Israelites hoarding up the manner when God had explicitly told them not to. I imagine it went something like this: Moses: “ God told you not to hoard it up or save any for later. Why do you disobey God’s direct command!?” Israelite: “ what reason could God possibly have for making it wrong to stock up food in the desert? I refuse to believe in God who would do that.”
  - IGNORANCE NON-SEQUITOR: but the Israelite has made an elementary logical error. Their inability to understand one of God’s Commandments has no impact on whether or not God exists. There are two completely separate questions.
    - RELEVANT TODAY: How many times have you heard this from non-Christians? I refuse to believe in the judgmental God, or a God, who would send people to hell, or a God who would tell people who they can and can’t love? They make the same non-sequitor error. You can say I don’t like a God who would make such a rule, or I don’t understand the rule, but you can’t say, since I don’t understand it, therefore that God does not exist.
  - MISUSING JESUS: Just as today, if you try to use the bread of heaven from Jesus selfishly and against the will of God, so that you do not have to have faith in God, Jesus’s body and blood will become a plague. **Those who choose to reject the bread from heaven that God offered so graciously become double sinners: sinners they always were, but now they are sinners who rejected the free gift of God.** And so they will decay, and become infested with sin, and stink to heaven.

**Exodus 16:22-30 > The First Sabbath**

- MIRACLE AMOUNTS: **Notice it’s not that the people intentionally gathered twice as much.** It’s that when they got back to measure what they gathered, which before was always miraculously an “omer”, today it was miraculously 2 omers. Only after they found they had twice as much did Moses tell them about the Sabbath.
- THE LORD HAS GIVEN YOU THE SABBATH: the Sabbath was created for man not man for the Sabbath, as Jesus said. It is truly a gift. To live life working seven days a week with no Sabbath is like being a slave. A well-paid slave, but a slave nonetheless. These slaves need a day to remind them that they are more than their work. Remember Pharaoh would not give them any time off. **Yahweh almost immediately gives them not only food, but a day where they can just rest and eat.** God truly does care about his chosen people!

**Exodus 16:31-end > Manna’s taste and jar for the Ark of the Covenant**

- DONUTS: raw, it tastes like wafers with honey. Numbers 11:8 tells us that when it is ground up and cooked, it tastes like cakes made with oil. What is a sweet cake fried in oil? Yep. A donut. God fed his people donuts from the sky, every day.
- MEMORY AGAIN: why keep a jar for generations? Why do we need to remember the Manna? To remind us God is about more than just miraculous breakfast. **Read John 6:16-58.** Manna, the bread from heaven, is a fractal with Jesus, the true bread from heaven. We put it in a jar in the ark so we will remember and recognize Jesus when he comes.
- MANNA ENDS AT CANAAN: the manna starts here 1 month into their journey, and ends 40 years later the day they first eat the produce of the promised land, never to happen again.

**Exodus 17:1-4 > Complaining at Moses**

- PEOPLE CANNOT DISTINGUISH MOSES FROM GOD: Dennis Prager made the point that the people seem unable to distinguish Moses from God. And this should not be surprising: he seems to have the power to just put up his hands and win a war, raise a stick and part the sea, strike a rock and get water. These are the visual things that people actually see when God‘s miracles are done. Because they can’t see God‘s hand. Because if you saw it, part of God, you would die. **So it makes perfect sense that the people who left a land whose king was part god, would expect that now their leader, who was doing greater miracles than any human had ever witnessed on earth, is also at least part God.** But Moses is the anti-Pharaoh: Moses constantly responds with the admonition that he is not God, and it is not by his power that these things are done except in Numbers with the strikes the rock one time and does not give God the credit. And that keeps him out of the promised land.

**Exodus 17:5-7 > Water from Rock**

- PASS BEFORE THE PEOPLE: When God told Moses to walk before the people to strike the rock, it had two potential meanings: to prove to Moses this they wouldn’t stone him (he was perhaps exaggerating), and to prove to the people that God is taking care of them through Moses.
- STAFF WITH WHICH YOU STRUCK THE NILE: when he struck the Nile, he turned it to blood and it became undrinkable. When he struck the rock, it poured forth water to a thirsty people as their salvation. Same staff, opposite effects.
  - SCIENCE DOESN’T WORK HERE: this specifically shows that the “staff” wasn’t some kind of magical Occult object. Spells and such things like that treated the spiritual world scientifically: you experiment, find a pattern that works consistently, then that becomes the incantation. This was all the magicians’ works in Egypt. **But here, the same guy with the same staff does basically opposite things.** Can’t experiment and draw conclusions. Yahweh is outside nature.
- ROCK WAS CHRIST: 1 Corinthians 10:4: “For they drank from the spiritual Rock that followed them, and the Rock was Christ.” Later, a sword strikes Jesus’s side, who was the rock, and water flowed out. From Christ, the Rock that followed them int he wilderness, pours the living water, and those who drink his blood and eat his flesh will never thirst or hunger again. **Jesus side flowing water is a fractal with Moses striking the rock.**

**Exodus 17:8-end > Amalekites attack**

- MAP: Amalekites were nomadic, so they move all over the map.
- HUR: Josephus tells us Hur was brother-in-law of Moses, the husband of Miriam
- PROMISED LAND COMEDY: https://youtu.be/HSI04-1oLwg?is=rILoPkwXUJJORRdS
- SITTING DUCKS CARRYING GOLD: why did the Amalekites attack? One reason could be as there is this massive state of recently freed slaves who were terrified to fight anybody, but were carrying most of Egypt’s gold with them. They have no fortifications, they have no defenses. And the Amalekites know the territory. Easy pickings.
- AMALEK ATTACKED THE STRAGGLERS: **Read Deuteronomy 25:17-19.**
- A MEMORIAL TO FORGET: this seems like a contradiction. Write a memorial that I will utterly blot out the memory of Amalek. And yet, we, 3500 years later, remember them when we read this now. I think God is doing a few things here: first, the Amalekites need to know how bad this was. Second, Israel needs to know that God takes this seriously. Third, the surrounding nations need to know that if they try what Amalek tried, they will be exterminated. And finally, he is instructing us to remember the evil that the Amalekites did, but not the Amalekites themselves. **We don’t know the names of any of them, and you can’t go find any today no matter how hard you look.** The only thing left of them is the memorial that Yahweh command commanded be written down.

- WHY DOESN’T GOD WIPE THEM OUT NOW? Why does Moses say Yahweh will have war with Amalek from generation to generation? That seems to be what happened. What’s the purpose?
  - GOD HAS A PLAN: He intends to test Israel with them later. Deuteronomy said they were to wipe them out after they settle the land. He commands it, and Saul fails, leading to Haman. So they were a big part of what happens later.
  - GROWTH OF ISRAEL: this is their first battle, and they win with Joshua leading and Moses hold up the hands. This represents graduation from Kindergarten to first grade. **They couldn’t wipe out the entire nation, because only raiding parties were hitting the rear.** If Yahweh had killed them all for them, then they wouldn’t achieve his goal of growing up into a people who can fight for themselves with Yahweh’s help. They would just start expecting Yahweh to do everything, and he seems to want them to do the work but rely on him for success. **So he can’t wipe them all out himself, and he couldn’t send them throughout the entire territory to hunt down every last Amalekite, because they are on their way to receive the law at Sinai.** So this will have to wait for another day.
  - AMALEK IS FIRST AMONG THE NATIONS TO ATTACK THE JEWS: We learned in the Song of Moses that the other nations learned of Yahweh and what he did in Egypt and they melted in fear. Amalek knew these were Yahweh’s people. **They knowingly attacked the chosen people of Yahweh.**
    - BALAAM: In Numbers 22, the king of Moab, Balaak, goes to a prophet of God named Balaam and asks him to curse Israel so he might be able to drive them out. Balaam takes Balaak’s money, and ends up giving him 4 “oracles”, or messages and predictions from God, and he keeps telling him that he cannot curse what Yahweh has blessed. But he loves Balaak’s money. So eventually, he tells Balaak that the only way to curse Israel is to entice them to sin so Yahweh himself punishes them. And he turns out to be right. But for that advice, he becomes one of the villains of history. But in one of the oracles at Numbers 24:20, he says this of the Amalekites: “**Then he \[Balaam\] looked upon Amalek and took up his discourse and said ‘Amalek was first among the nations, but its end is utter destruction**.”
    - FIRST AMONG NATIONS: In what way were the Amalekites first among the nations? What did they do first, before anybody else? The attacked the Jews. They were the first to attack the people whom Yahweh was explicitly leading. And as such, Amalek became a symbol for any nation who refused the rule of Yahweh, who attacks the Jews. **And so one meaning of God’s statement is the double meaning that Amalek will be utterly wiped out from the Earth, but he will be at war with all the people who attack his people from generation to generation, of whom Amalek was the first prototype.**
