

**Summary: Exodus 12:21-12:51**

**Title:** **The blood and body of the slaughtered Passover lamb shield the Israelites from Yahweh's killing of the firstborn that leads Pharaoh to order them to get out of Egypt. God commands eternal memory of Passover in rituals that point directly to Jesus.**

**Storyline Summary:**

- **Process for killing the lamb: Instructions for the Big Night**
- **They must tell their children about this night forever**
- **Firstborn of Egypt die**
- **Israel gets paid with Egypt’s gold**
- **Pharaoh kicks them out**
- **Ready to head out**
