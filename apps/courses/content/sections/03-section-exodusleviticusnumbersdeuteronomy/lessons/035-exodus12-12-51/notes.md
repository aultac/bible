# 035_Exodus12-12:51

- FRACTAL WITH REVELATION 16: **Read Revelation 16**. The seven bowl judgments. Water to blood, boils, frogs, hail, darkness. In the seven trumpet judgments in Revelation 9, we also get supernatural locusts from the pit of hell. Big fractal. We talked about how unexpected it was that God introduced himself to the broader world through a series of plagues. God says hello with plagues. **Now we see that this is also how God will say goodbye: two sets of seven plagues to finish off the Earth.** Hello and goodbye, bookends on the whole show.
- RECAPTURE DRAMA: Pharaoh tells Moses he’s going to kill him if he ever sees him again. Moses is very angry and says “FINE!” But he turns and gives Pharaoh one last message from Yahweh. **Re-read Chapter 11:4-end.** The creator of the universe just told Moses he is coming and it will be terrible. We are at the climax of this very dramatic story. The suspense has been building for weeks. Yahweh just took down the supreme Egyptian god Ra, the sun god.

**12:1-6 > Rules for Calendar and Passover Lamb**

- WHY BREAK AT THE MOST DRAMATIC POINT? Dennis Prager made this point. Literarily, this is the worst place to break for a set of commandments to the Hebrews. The tension has been building. It has reached its climax. **And now, a short interlude of rules.** It must be important enough to the point God is making here to interrupt the central drama as if this, actually, is the central issue. And what is his first command? What is the issue so central that it supersedes the storyline’s central drama? **Memory. God is telling us that the Hebrews need to understand that he is not taking them out of Egypt until they know that they will have to remember this moment for eternity.** These are in fact, the only rules given to Israel in Egypt. The important part of this entire story is actually the ongoing ritual. More text will be spent on Passover than on the actual killing of the firstborn.
  - FREEDOM IS SECONDARY: the story is trying to tell us that the freedom of the Hebrews was important but secondary. **Their purpose in all this is to be the conduit through which the world will learn who Yahweh is.** If simply their freedom was paramount, then the story would have just continued right on showing how the first born died and they made it out of Egypt. Then God could have given them a feast to remember this by once they were out in the desert and free. **But the way he did it here, not everyone will actually be taken to freedom.** Only those who followed God‘s command, and put their faith in him through the lambs blood on the door. Freedom was secondary. He will not take them out of Egypt until he shows them that the purpose of their freedom is to worship him. To be his priest to the nations through their rituals. To keep the memory of the Passover alive for all eternity.
  - MEMORY IS WHO YOU ARE: I once heard an author interviewed on a book called “my life, deleted”. He was a middle-aged man who slipped on the bathroom floor at work one morning, hit his head on the floor, and got one of the worst cases of retrograde amnesia on record. It was absolutely fascinating. He had not recovered any of his former memory even after years. He described it as understanding what a father was, but not remembering that he was a father. He knew her husband was, but he did not remember that he was a husband. He had all the same molecules in him as before, but his wife and children say he is a completely different person. There are some mannerisms and a few habits that stuck around. **But his children basically have an adoptive father. His wife had to learn how to love someone else.**
  - WE ARE OUR MEMORIES: and it just struck me hard that day that **I am my memories.** **Take away my memories, and I am not me anymore. Your memories are your life.** And that is why when you spend time doing things that shut your brain down and put you in auto pilot, where you just pass time like a dazed zombie, you killed yourself for those hours. You lost a little bit of your life because there is no memory. You are effectively asleep. This is what happens when you mindlessly scroll your phone. This is what happens when you watch television. You have taken that little bit of your life away, voluntarily killing that part of you, which could’ve been a memory .
  - MEMORY MAKES NATIONS: And it is the same with nations. Israel is not Israel without its memory. The United States is not the United States when we forget how we got here. And that has happened before your very eyes. Since World War 2, every generation has remembered US History less and less. And now, very few people know our history and even less value it.
  - GOD ACTS ONCE AND EXPECTS US TO REMEMBER: God does not intend to keep doing the same thing over and over and over in history. History is a fractal so we can see his hand in it, but it is not cyclical. **He intends to act once and then your job is to remember it for the rest of time.**  We may wish that God would work miracles in every generation so we have constant proof of his existence. But the God we are learning about hear in Exodus tells us that he is not like that. He intends that when he does a miracle, we are supposed to remember it and learn about it from those who came before us.
  - ATHEISTS CLAIM HISTORY CANNOT BE KNOWN: have you ever watched an atheist debate the historical evidence for Christianity? They get trapped into not trusting anything they didn’t see with their own eyes. Because if you accept there was a Roman Empire, you accept it through the writing of others. **If you saw an actual miracle, you would definitely want to write it down so the other people knew about it.** And so they end up in this strange position of either applying a completely different standard of evidence to the historic work of Christianity or they simply claim that you can’t know history at all. And then they write books about it so that other people in the future will learn that you can’t know history at all, especially from the writings of others. **You can only know what you see with your own two eyes.** Can you imagine anything more foolish than claiming you should be incapable of learning from the lives and experiences of others?
  - WHAT HAVE YOU DONE FOR ME LATELY: Dennis Prager pointed out that a very strong teaching in Judaism around the Exodus is that every single Jew is supposed to **see themselves as if they personally were rescued out of Egypt**. If you have no memory, if you do not study the Exodus, then you walk around accusing God of not acting in the world. **Because you refuse to read, learn and ingest all the times that he already acted in the world you walk around with a “what have you done for me lately” sort of attitude toward God.** If you don’t remember, you can’t be grateful. God deserves to have his actions in history remembered.
  - OWE MEMORY TO VICTIMS: God is actually very concerned about remembering the pain of the Egyptians in the first born who died. It was one of the revelations of my life when **I realized that all the victims of the great evils of the world that I saw all had the same desire in life: for other people to know about what they went through.** How many movies do you see where the actual end of the movie where everything is made right is simply that the secret evil has finally made it onto the news and everyone would know about it now. But when I saw that, I realized that if the victims want someone to know, then those who were not victims have a duty to learn about it. **And that means reading about and remembering the awful things humanity has done so that the pain and injustice done to the victims is not forgotten.** If you were in that position, I can promise you it’s the least you would want, that the world would know what it happened to you. And so God commands memory here so the first born of Egypt will never be forgotten. And the firstborn of the Hebrews who were thrown in the river will also never be forgotten.
  - THE PRIMARY REASON JEWS STILL EXIST: is because of this ceremony commanded by God. There is no reason of all the ancient peoples around at the time here in Genesis and Exodus that the Jews should still be around. There are no Hitittes, no Babylonians, no Amalekites, no Edomites, no Philistines. Instead, there is a single people who didn’t even have a homeland for 2000 years. If your children move to France, within a few generations they will be French. And the primary reason the Jews still exist is this ceremony right here, the Passover (and the Sabbath.) They get together with their families this one time per year to remember. Every Jew is taught to see themselves as personally escaping from Egypt. Because of what we are reading right now.
  - CATHEDRAL IN TIME: Abraham Joshua Herschel wrote a book in 1951 titled “The Sabbath.” In it, he has the tremendous insight that every other religion, every other god, every other nation creates cathedrals in space. Buildings. But the God of the Torah, Yahweh, created his cathedrals in time: the Sabbath and the Passover. As he said, **cathedrals in time “… that neither the Romans nor the Germans were able to burn.”** Yahweh is a God of memory.
  - WHAT KIND OF RITUAL WORKS BEST? So if you want to create a ritual that ensures memory of an event for 3500 years, what properties should that ritual have? What works best? The Jews have the answer for us. Nothing anywhere from any group exists today like Passover does for the Jews. **If you want to remember, your ritual must engage all 5 physical senses and your mind.** Sight, hearing (breaking matzo, singing, recitation of questions), taste (bitter herbs, wine, matzo, delicious meal), touch (washing hands, cracker-like matzo), smell (roasted meat). And for your mind, it must engage the children so they care and it must explain to everyone present the purpose of the holiday.
  - CONTRAST THIS WITH JULY 4: contrast this with our July 4 ritual. Do we explain to our children every year what made America distinctive? We eat hot dogs and hamburgers. We get a day off work. We watch fireworks. We smell the marijuana at the city fireworks. But our sights, sounds, tastes, touches, and smells do not explain to us our heritage. We do not explain America. And so our children do not know why we are here. They do not know what America is because they have no memory of the nation. We will not outlast the Jews. Our measly 250 years is a drop in the bucket in the history of the Jews. Their rituals beat our rituals.
  - THIS IS WHY: so this is why we break at the most dramatic point. **Because remembering Passover was the point of the whole thing. I cannot stress this enough.** The ultimate purpose of the plagues was the eternal remembrance of Passover. The center of the story at the most dramatic point is instructions to remember Passover forever. **Apparently the actual memory of Passover is going to be almost singularly important in the entire history of the world.**
- CALENDAR: this one event changed even how the Israelites reckon time for the rest of eternity. There are actually two Jewish calendars: the one they already had at this time where harvest is the new year for civil and agricultural things, and this new calendar starting in the spring for festivals and religious activities. They don’t really celebrate Nissan 1 as the new year in the spring though, they celebrate the new year as Rosh Shashanah in the fall (1st of month of Tishrei).
  - LUNISOLAR CALENDAR: lunar-based months of 29.5 days (some 29, some 30), adding “leap month” of Adar 7 out of 19 years to sync up with solar calendar so festivals don’t move out of their seasons.
- LAMB MUST ARRIVE FOUR DAYS BEFORE PASSOVER: on the 10th of the month of Nissan. Deuteronomy 16, God instructed Israel that they must bring the lamb four days before the Passover and have it approved by the priests at the temple.
  - WHY KEEP LAMB FOUR DAYS? Dennis Prager points out that up to now God had made no requirements on the Hebrews. They were simply his chosen people with no instruction. Understand that sacrifices were primarily about eating. Unlike in our modern culture when an animal loses its life for us to eat we are completely shielded from that, there is no sense of the animal when you order a Big Mac, **in the sacrificial system you had to acknowledge the loss of life prior to enjoying the food.**
  - DELAYED GRATIFICATION: So the family must gather the lamb and wait with mouth watering for four days before they could actually eat it. And the only reason they would be waiting four days is purely because God commanded it. Similar to how we pray to God before we eat a meal, instead of digging in right away, we pause to acknowledge our creator, who blessed us with the meal. **This becomes a test of willpower: do not eat this now solely because Yahweh said to wait.** Which is exactly the kind of a thing slaves would need as they prepare to face freedom.   **And make no mistake, freedom in the end is their greatest adversary.** They are very much about to “face” freedom just as David will one day “face” Goliath.
  - LAMB SEEN BY EGYPTIANS FORCES SLAVES TO TAKE A STAND: in the first Passover, they had to tie the lamb up in their house. Their Egyptian neighbors would have seen it and all understood what was happening. **It was a powerful test of faith for the Hebrews.** They had to be more afraid of failing to heed Yahweh‘s commands than they were of their Egyptian neighbors seeing them tie up their gods for a sacrifice. **It would also be powerful for the Egyptians, who had begun to question if they should be following their own gods or Yahweh instead.** To see all of the Hebrews tying up the lambs in preparation for four days, it gave anyone who wanted to have faith in Yahweh the opportunity to join in. We learn later that a very large number of Egyptians actually joined this cause, and went out with the Hebrews worshiping Yahweh as a mixed multitude.

**12:7-13 > Eat the lamb, it’s blood will protect you**

- KILL AND EAT EGYPT’S GODS: and what you don’t eat, burn before the night is out. Their gods are done hurting you by morning. This would have been an incredibly powerful act at dinner. To sacrifice a god to our God. And it would be dangerous. Moses said earlier the Egyptians would stone them if they sacrificed like this in Egypt.
- LAMB’S BLOOD IS WHAT MATTERED, NOT THAT YOU WERE HEBREW: if a Hebrew did not put lamb’s blood on the door, what happened to them? Would they be saved from the coming punishment for sin of the death of the firstborn? What about if an Egyptian trusted Yahweh and put the lamb’s blood on the door? Were they saved? We find out yes, they were, when the Bible tells us a mixed multitude went out of Egypt, not only Hebrews. **So we learn from the God of the 10 plagues that the Hebrews deserved the death of the firstborn as well.** **Remember the Hebrew beating his own kinsman?** He did not pick them because of their own awesomeness: he picked them because they were weak and through them he could enact this story. These 10 plagues to show us he hates evil enough to punish it. So we learn here that we’re all sinners, and we all deserve death, but if we just put an unblemished lamb’s blood on the door that was sacrificed to Yahweh, and eat it’s body as signs that we trust Yahweh, then we will be saved from the natural punishment for our sin which is death. **And if he picks us, but we do not trust him enough to use the blood of the lamb and eat its body, then we will not be saved.**
    - DOUBLE SINNERS IGNORED GOD’S GIFT: not only would a Hebrew who did not put the lambs blood on their door have their first born taken by the angel of death, **they were doubly a sinner because they willingly ignored the gift of the lamb that God offered them as a way to avoid his judgment on Egypt.** God it does not take lightly the fact that he has to come down and kill the firstborn. The saving of the Hebrew first born is like rain on the crops: the rain falls on the just and unjust alike. The Hebrews are commanded to understand and remember throughout all their generations that the first born of Egypt or precious to God, and the Hebrews are not supposed to take for granted that these people, men, women and children, all died as a part of the process to obtain their freedom and redemption. **Not because they deserved it, as if they earned their way into God‘s favor through the work done by the Egyptians to torture them.** They were not saved because they would have done any differently if they were in the Egyptian shoes, and not because they were the most oppressed people who had ever lived. **They were saved because God chose them and offered them a free gift of freedom but they had to accept it by eating the body of the lamb and covering their door with its blood.**
    - HOLY SEPARATE PEOPLE: Yahweh is beginning the process of making the Hebrews a distinct, separate people from the Egyptians and from every other nation on Earth. And the primary sign that he gives them to see their “separateness” (holiness) is the blood of the lamb. It is the blood of this lamb that is the outward sign to both man and Yahweh that they are separate from Egypt, that they are holy.

**12:14-20 > Future feast**

- PASSOVER AND FEAST OF UNLEAVENED BREAD: two separate events, but the feast starts the day after Passover. It is a celebration that God saved our children when he came to kill the first born. If a non-Israelite wants to celebrate Passover, they have strict rules. But if Israelites DO NOT want to celebrate the Feast of Unleavened Bread, they have strict consequences for failing to do so (failing by eating leaven.)
- WHY LEAVEN? Later in the text it mentions the lack of leaven is a sign of leaving in haste. So that is definitely a component. But that doesn’t explain this feast of unleavened bread. They had at least 4 days to prepare for the Passover, which was plenty of time for bread to rise. It was only during the actual flight prior to the Red Sea that they didn’t have time to stop for bread to rise.
  - MODERN JEWS: modern Jews hide leavened bread in the house for the kids to find on this day and then ceremonially burn it to represent removing the leaven from their house.
  - WHAT’S IN THE WORLD IS WRONG WITH LEAVEN? Dennis Prager made the point that if I asked you to come up with a practice that would ensure an event was remembered for the next 3500 years, your first idea would probably not be “Ban yeast.“ What is wrong with yeast? And so wrong that the punishment for eating it is to be cut off from your people? As Prager pointed out, it sounds like capital punishment for jaywalking.
  - BURN ALL LEAVEN AND NOTHING FOR 7 DAYS IS NOT HASTE: You must not eat it for 7 days. How does that symbolize leaving in haste? It’s not that you don’t have time for the bread to rise, you have to cleanse your entire house of leaven.
  - LEAVEN REPRESENTS DEATH: throughout the Bible leaven represents sin and death. Leaven itself comes from fermenting or rotting things. When things die, the yeast starts to work. It corrupts the formerly living thing and rots it . And, like the corruption of sin, it spreads through the entire body. **And these people are leaving Egypt, a civilization that reveled in death and the afterlife.** Their greatest monuments in history are tombs. They aspire to be Osiris, the god of the underworld. Their most revered religious text is called “The Book of the Dead.” As we will see when we go through the rest of the Torah, Yahweh directs most of his ritual and laws to teach them to choose life and not death. **Yahweh is the God of Life. The death cult of Egypt must be ritually purified out of them**. And it starts right here with the ban on leaven. Banning leaven means seeking life.
  - DISTINCTION: Remember, Yahweh kills the firstborn so that the Egyptians will know that Yahweh makes a distinction between Egypt and Israel. And Israel needs ritual to feel and practice the distinction. This ritual is the beginning of the “distinct from Egypt” training that Yahweh is going to put Israel through. Leaven, decay, slavery, and death are part of Egypt. Life, growth, and liberty are part of Yahweh.
- WHAT DOES “CUT-OFF” MEAN? Modern commentators say it means expulsion from the community, like excommunication. But the more ancient scholars like the rabbis in the Talmud argue it is a God-delivered punishment, either as premature death in this world, or loss of a portion in the world to come, like losing your salvation. **I think it means you are no longer a child of the promise because you rejected Yahweh.** You threw your lot in with Esau, not Jacob.
  - SINS AGAINST GOD: Skipping Passover is one of the 19 things listed in the entire Bible that can cause you to be “cut off from your people.” It uses the Hebrew word here for soul, so it is not just something in this life. It literally says his soul will be cut off. Of the 19 things, they are all sins against God. None of them are sins against man. You are never cut off from your people for sins against man. Dennis Prager points out that, critically, this means man does not carry out this sentence. God does. Man does not put to death the one who skips Passover. But God will handle it in the life to come when he will no longer be a child of the promise.
  - PUNISHMENT OR CONSEQUENCE? Dennis Prager also pointed out that if you buy a new device and you refuse to read the manual, or you read the manual and you refuse to do what the manual says, is it really punishment when you can’t make the device work like it’s supposed to? Part of being cut off is that you are cut off from God’s promise. **God gave you a manual, and if you don’t read it, or if you don’t do what it says for salvation, is it really punishment when you don’t get salvation?** If he says I give you a free gift that you can be saved from your sins. All you have to do is repent and trust in me, and you do not repent and trust in him, are you being punished when you don’t get the salvation? This is how non-Christians see our religion: they think we believe that everyone who is not a Christian is going to hell because they are not a Christian. They don’t understand that they are going to hell because they are a sinner. They simply aren’t escaping hell because they are not a Christian.
