

**Summary: Exodus 13-15:18**

**Title:** **Yahweh commands the consecration of the firstborn and feast of unleavened bread. Yahweh went with them by the pillar both day and night. Pharaoh pursued after the Israelites which led to their destruction during their attempt at crossing the Red Sea, then they sang a song praising Yahweh for his faithfulness.**

**Storyline Summary:**

- **Consecration of firstborn commanded**
- **More details on Feast of Unleavened Bread**
- **Tefillin/phylacteries**
- **Pillar of cloud and fire**
- **Cross the desert toward the Red Sea following the pillar because they aren’t ready for battling Philistines on the Via Maris**
- **Joseph’s bones taken out with Israel**
- **Pharaoh forgets the plagues and heads after them**
- **Red Sea crossing: from trapped to saved**
- **Song of Moses**
