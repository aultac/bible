

**Summary: Exodus 18-19:15**

**Title:** **Jethro, Moses’ father in law, visits and gives Moses the idea to select good men to help administer minor judgements for God's people. God asks the people to obey Him, they agree and then God begins the introduction of His law.**

**Storyline Summary:**

- **Jethro comes to visit**
- **Zipporah and the kids came too**
- **Moses tells Jethro everything that has happened**
- **Jethro rejoices and sacrifices to Yahweh**
- **Jethro goes to work with Moses: take your father-in-law to work day**
- **Jethro tells Moses he is doing it wrong: judging by himself isn’t going to scale**
- **Jethro teaches the power of delegation and sets the foundation for the Western judiciary system**
- **Judges need four qualities: able, honest, no bribes, God-fearing.**
- **Do not touch the mountain**
- **Take a bath and wash your clothes**
- **People signed the contract without reading it: we will keep your commands**
- **Yahweh tells Israel your job is to be priests to the nations**
