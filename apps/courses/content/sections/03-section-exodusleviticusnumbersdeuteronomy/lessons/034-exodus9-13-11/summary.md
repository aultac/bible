

**Summary: Exodus 9:13-11**

**Title:** **God sends destructive hail, devouring locust and heavy darkness upon Egypt. Pharaoh continues to lie to get his way but his heart continues to be hardened. God's final plague is revealed so a distinction will be made between Egypt and His Israel.**

**Storyline Summary:**

- **HAIL**
- **Pharaoh lies again**
- **Locusts clean up anything green leftover**
- **Pharaoh says Ra sets his face against you**
- **Heavy darkness that you can feel as an attack on the supreme Egyptian god of the sun, Ra**
- **Final plague is threatened. Firstborn will die. Everybody in the land will lose their firstborn, to show a distinction between Egypt and Israel.**
