Jesus’s resurrection is not natural. **Nothing about our religion is natural.** That is why today’s worshippers of nature cannot see the truth. Because they worship nature. If it is not natural, it is not true. This is their foundational belief. **That statement itself is not natural, so they take it on faith.** Pain is natural. Nature is red in tooth and claw. The young and the infirm die. That is natural. The strong take everything they want. That is natural. The weak serve the strong. The stranger is to be mistreated to serve my group. That is natural. Loving the stranger is completely unnatural.

Disease kills us. We are controlled by nature. It is natural to worship nature. When we in modernity control nature, harnessing its power to serve us, then we worship ourselves along with nature. Or we worship the process we used to control nature, science. We say prayers to our net and worship our dragnet, as Habakkuk told us, for by them we live in comfort. **So it is natural to worship nature either because we control it for our comfort, or because it controls us in its immensity.**
- THE ATHEIST DOES NOT DISAGREE WITH THIS: This is what the worshipers of science say. Only the natural is true. Only that which can be test tested through repeated experimentation is true. Only nature is deserving of worship and study. This is the scientific world we live in. **It has chosen to worship nature and forgotten the God of the plagues.**
- EGYPT’S GODS WERE NATURAL: they were depicted as animals. They are subject to laws and battles with other gods. They can grant you knowledge that lets you control nature through occult rituals and spells. **But they are as much a part of nature as we are.** As one Hindu convert to Christianity said, he began his journey to Christ when we realized that the gods of Hinduism were all subject to nature, specifically to samsara, the cycle of birth, death, and rebirth. The gods themselves were always forever stuck in the wheel! Even the gods cannot escape samsara! Jesus is not stuck in nature, Yahweh is not stuck in nature.
- GOD IS HOLY: God first shows humanity he is not natural. In fact, he controls the natural. He is wholly separate from nature. He is holy. There is nothing natural about him. We have lived in a Christian society governed by the unnatural Christian religion so we forget that morality is not natural. Care for the weak and the fatherless is not natural. Even the western atheist forgets this because they benefit from the unnatural Christian civilization created by the unnatural God. They deluded themselves into thinking Christian goodness is natural. **In reality it exists nowhere in the world except where the unnatural God who created the universe is worshiped.** And it only exists imperfectly here! Nowhere on Earth is like the United States. Nowhere.
- EVERYONE’S CHOICE: Every human who has ever lived makes a choice between worshiping nature, worshipping themselves, or worshiping the God outside of nature.
- DO NOT ALLOW NATURE TO CONTROL YOU: this God who is outside of nature demands that we be holy because he is holy. In showing us that there is something outside of nature that controls it, he is separate or “holy,” **he shows us that we are not to be controlled by our own natures. We are not to act naturally.** We are to ask him for help to enact 10 plagues on our sin that is keeping us as slaves. To be holy and wholly separate from nature.
- YOUR PRIMARY BATTLE IN LIFE IS WITH YOUR NATURE: it is only this God who shows us in fact that our primary battle in life is with our nature. That is a fundamentally Judeo-Christian idea that came from the God of the 10 plagues. **Far from our nature being something to aspire to, it is in fact our primary enemy in our everyday lives.** It is in fact, powerful proof that Christianity is the most accurate description of reality, that it is “true:” you know in your own life it is your own nature that you fight the most. The influence of Satan in the world tells you that you must discover yourself, your truest self is when you have found your hidden nature and give into it. People go on quests to find themselves. **Christianity says you don’t want to find yourself, you stink.** You want to find God, there is none good but him. You want to follow his nature, not the nature inside of you. The unnatural God.
- OPPOSITE OF THE LGBTQ MESSAGE: the message of the world is you are your nature. If your nature is same sex attraction, then that is what you are. If your nature is to feel like you are in the wrong gendered body, simply declare you are another gender and mutilate yourself to fit your nature. It is natural. **This God shows you through 10 plagues that there is in fact something outside your nature and he can control your nature.**  He can inspire fear, repentance, or even give you back your free will by strengthening your heart. Just ask him.
- SECOND GREAT REVELATION OF THE PLAGUES: universal moral law. Not only does Yahweh exist, and he controls nature, but **he expects all of humanity to adhere to a universal moral code.**
  - RIGHT AND WRONG VS. POWER: And that code includes humane treatment of the stranger in your midst. And might does not make right: the powerful do not determine morality. God does. **Nearly every place in the world today that is not part of Christendom sees the powerful as the determinators of right and wrong.**  They respect only strength, weakness is to be degraded. The God of the plagues says the might of Egypt has been turned toward evil. The moral compass did not turn with them: what they did was evil, regardless of their strength.
  - UNIVERSAL NOT REGIONAL OR COMMUNITY-BASED: The Egyptians do not live in their own isolated region with their own isolated gods, and therefore can do whatever is allowed by their community or their regional gods. In contrast to the false wisdom of our postmodern age, it turns out morality is not determined by the community. The Egyptians at this point have voted to keep the Hebrews enslaved. **And the God of the plagues says that is wrong whether you voted for it or not.**
  - EVERY HUMAN HAS A CONSCIENCE: This is something that when you see it in the Bible, you know it inherently, and yet it was completely revolutionary in the world. Without God, there is no universal moral code. But this God who brings the plagues says **if you do evil anywhere in the world, you have broken my law and I will know about it.**  And there will be judgment and punishment.
  - LOVE THE GOD WHO PUNISHES EVIL: And I love him for that even if the punishment includes me. We should all be praying to God each day. Thank you for being good and hating evil, even my own evil, in fact, thank you for enabling us to identify good and evil at all.
- THIRD REVELATION NON-MATERIAL REALITY: it was not a revelation to the Egyptians, but it is to us today. The plagues, if true, prove to us there is a non-material element to reality.
  - NON-EMPIRICAL KNOWLEDGE: There are things that cannot be known empirically. We cannot test God to see if the same 10 plagues always happen in the same repeated experiment. The nature worshipers of today claim there is no truth outside of the empirically verifiable: **they will only believe what can be proved scientifically.** They are completely blind to the fact that they believe that statement, and it cannot be proved empirically.
  - BASIS OF ALL ABSTRACT THOUGHT: They are blind to the fact that nearly everything of importance to them is abstract and non-material: mathematics, the laws of logic, love, morality. They cannot touch it, smell it, feel it, or measure it. None of these things live in the physical world. **It is in fact, the belief in the non-material that enables abstract thought at all.** It is the plagues that prove the basis of mathematics, laws of logic and reason. As **Stephen Meyer** has often pointed out something like this based on Stephen Hawking’s statement that if there is such a thing as a law of gravity, then the universe can and will create itself out of nothing. But “a law of gravity” is not nothing. What is a law? Where does it live? It is an abstract concept, especially if there is no material reality around in which for it to exist. **Abstract concepts only exist in minds**, so the idea that the laws of the universe created the universe is actually an argument for God’s existence because it means there was a mind outside the material universe that conceived and held those laws while the material universe was being created.
  - PLAGUES PUT THAT QUESTION TO REST: And this God of the plagues is putting the question to bed once and for all. There is a non-material component of reality. **God is proving that he cannot be experimented on and measured like nature.** He is his own agent. You cannot create another Egypt and see if the same 10 plagues happen, experimentation with God does not work. I’m sure Pharaoh was thinking this: everybody has slaves. Everybody is harsh on their slaves. Why am I the one getting the plagues?

**9:13-end > Plague 7: Hail**

- GOD HUMILIATES PHARAOH BEFORE HIS PEOPLE: pharaoh has to be the one to warn them. Imagine how it would have sounded to the people: “Pharaoh, your god and king, commands you stay in doors because Yahweh is about to rain hail on the entire land and kill anything that is outside.”
- PHARAOH MUST HAVE STAYED INSIDE: because clearly he did not die.
- GOD DOES NOT WANT TO HURT THE EGYPTIANS: he warns them so they have a chance. They need only trust in the word of Yahweh and they will be saved.
- WHERE DID LIVESTOCK COME FROM? Previous plague was only Egyptian livestock out in the field. Also unclear what timeframe was between then and now: could have bought more since then.
- MOSES DOESN’T NEED AARON NOW: he has come a long way. He has full confidence now in Yahweh, and that his only real qualification for the job now is that Yahweh is with him.
- MOSES DID NOT PLEAD WITH GOD: Pharaoh asked him to plead, but he just went out and raised his hands and the hail stopped. That must’ve had a big impact on pharaoh and his courtiers.

**10:1-20 > Plague 8: Locusts**

- RA SETS HIS FACE AGAINST YOU: since the Hebrew word for evil (Ra) is the same as the Egyptian name of the all powerful Sun god Ra, several commentators have pointed out the play on words in the Hebrew here that pharaoh says Moses has set his face toward evil or is bent on evil, but **it equivalently says that Ra has set his face against you**. If that is true, it would be incredibly appropriate that the next plague after the locusts is darkness.
- I HAVE SINNED AGAINST YOUR GOD: Dennis Prager points out he is not saying that he sinned objectively, just against Moses’s God. **He is not recognizing that he did anything wrong within his culture or his gods, he is just recognizing power.** Moses’s God clearly has the power, and he is not happy with Pharaoh. That’s all he means. He is not repentant as if he actually did anything wrong. Yahweh is just mad at him.
- GODS ASSOCIATED WITH THIS PLAGUE:
  - Osiris: god of agriculture
  - Renenutet: goddess of protecting the harvest specifically.

**10:21-end > Plague 9: Darkness**

- FOG, DUST, or DARK: it is unclear what dark That you can feel means. It could be like a dark fog or a dust storm, but it doesn’t say dust storm. It seems to me more like a description of darkness in a cave.
- GODS ASSOCIATED WITH THIS PLAGUE:
  - Amun-Ra: “highest god”, the sun god, syncretism between “mysterious, unknowable” Amon and sun god Ra.   **Imagine the comedy of the hundreds of Amun-Ra priests of the Sun god blundering around in the dark during the day with no sun.**
  - Ra-Horakhty: syncretism between Horus and Ra. Eye of Horus was the sun. Hey Pharoah, where’s the sun?
  - Ra-Khepri: scarab beetle god rolling the sun across the sky each day. Apparently he was sleeping today.
- I WILL NOT SEE YOUR FACE AGAIN: and now it all reaches a head. Pharoah has made a promise that he intends to keep this time: Moses will die if he sees him again. This made Moses angry, and he tells Pharoah “As you say!”, but before he leaves he gives them the message of the final plague of Yahweh, coming up in chapter 11.

**11:1-end > Threat of Firstborn’s Death**

- TERRIFYINGLY DRAMATIC: when Yahweh himself says I will enter Egypt, is there anything more terrifying in all the Earth? This is one of the most dramatic moments in all the human history. When Moses stands before pharaoh and says Yahweh himself is coming.
- CHRONOLOGY PROBLEM: this seems to happen in the same conversation when Pharaoh first says you won’t see my face again. Then we get a flashback to Moses learning the full plan from Yahweh earlier when he told them to collect gold from their neighbors. Then the narrative returns to the current situation where he gives his last words to Pharaoh after Pharaoh has told him never to show his face again. **So Moses once again hears Yahweh in his mind as he turns to leave, so he turns back around and tells Pharaoh midnight is when the firstborn will die.** Then Chapter 12 flashes back again to the start of the month 14 days ago when God had commanded Passover in preparation.
- A GREAT CRY: when God says something is the greatest that has ever happened, and the greatest that will ever happen, you know it’s not just hyperbole. It is hard to imagine the sound that is about to assault the ears on the upcoming night. Everyone has a firstborn, and for those with a mom or dad who was also a firstborn, they would have more than one death. Not just a lot of mom’s and dad’s screaming. Not most people screaming. **Every. Single. Household. Every single living mom and dad**. And brother and sister. And not just a movie scream. Not a terrified scream. Screams of loss from the depths of the soul. The scream you have when you come home and find your child has fallen off a balcony when you were away and their lifeless crumpled body was laying on the ground, dead. That kind of scream. Times millions. Purdue’s football stadium holds 61,000 people. Ever been in a packed football stadium when it was yelling? Imaging them all screaming with anguish of the soul instead of excitement. **Then multiply that by 50 to get to \~3 million Egyptians.**
- THAT YOU MAY KNOW I MAKE A DISTINCTION: God said this back in Exodus 8:33 also when the first “distinction” plague happened (the flies/scarabs). He doesn’t say why he makes the distinction or what the distinction means. It seems to be critical to the story, though: the last plague’s purpose is illustrating this distinction. So what does it mean?
  - CHOSEN VS NOT CHOSEN: First, from the text thus far, we know it means the people who are not chosen by Yahweh must be taught that Yahweh makes a distinction between peoples, specifically between the people he chose and the people he did not choose, not based on their works. **People who do not know Yahweh need to know there is a judgment, a heaven and a hell, and Yahweh makes a distinction between humans.**
  - AGGRESSOR VS VICTIM: Second, Yahweh makes a distinction between the aggressor, Egypt, and the victim, Israel. **Yahweh came down in the first place when he heard the cries of his people.**  From this point forward, Yahweh will bless those who bless Israel and curse those who curse Israel. But if Israel chooses not to be his people, they will go back across the divide of the distinction and be punished just like the aggressor just for the sin of rejecting him, his grace, and his goodness.
  - PUNISHMENT AND PROTECTION: Third, the illustration of that distinction is pain at the mercy of nature’s forces being supernaturally controlled, culminating in the loss of their firstborn. **The distinction could not be more stark:** Yahweh’s chosen people have a normal, pain-free night without even a dog barking, and everyone else raises an involuntary cry so loud it has never been heard before, and likely will never be heard again until judgment day. The day on which God will make a distinction between the sheep and the goats. **The world must know that Yahweh makes a distinction between Egypt and Israel.**
