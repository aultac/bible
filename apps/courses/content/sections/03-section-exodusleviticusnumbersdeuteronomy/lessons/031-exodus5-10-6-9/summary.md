

**Summary: Exodus 5:10-6:9**

**Title:** **After meeting with Moses, Pharoah takes away the straw so Israelites cannot make bricks and beats them for it. Hebrew foremen blame Moses, Moses blames God, God promises their deliverance.**

**Storyline Summary:**

- **Taskmasters and foremen tell Hebrews no straw, but keep quota!**
- **Foremen complained to Pharoah, Pharoah says they are just lazy**
- **Nobody blames Pharoah**
- **Moses accuses God of evil directly: Why have you done this?!**
- **Just wait and see, 7 eternal promises to take them out of slavery and be their God.**
