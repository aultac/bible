

**Summary: Exodus 19:16-20:2**

**Title:** **The Israelites trembled as God made a fearful display of his presence through the smoke, thunder, lightning, trumpet blasts and threat of death for touching the mountain before reminding them that He is the one who delivered them out of their slavery in Egypt.**

**Storyline Summary:**

- **God is on Sinai on the third day**
- **Moses brought the people out to meet Yahweh**
- **Moses spoke and God answered in thunder**
- **God calls Moses up the mountain. Again.**
- **God tells Moses to go back down to warn the people again not to touch the mountain.**
- **God tells Moses to bring Aaron back up with him**
- **And God spoke all these words saying:**
- **I am Yahweh your Elohim who took you out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of slavery.**
