

**Summary: Exodus 1:17-2:15**

**Title:** **The midwives feared God and the Hebrews multiplied despite pharaoh's efforts. Moses is spared and is raised by pharaoh’s daughter. Moses later fights evil and then flees to Midian.**

**Storyline Summary:**

- **Midwives, Shiphrah and Puah, feared God allowed the boys to live**
- **Moses went on a cruise in his own little ark**
- **Moses is raised by Pharoah’s daughter (and his mom was his nurse)**
- **Moses murdered the Egyptian killing the Hebrew**
- **Moses speaks up to the Hebrew oppressing his brother.**
- **Moses flees to Midian, sits down by the well.**
