

**Summary: Exodus 3:13-4:17**

**Title:** **Moses continues to question God’s plan of using him to deliver the Israelites out of Egypt and God gives Moses 3 miracles to use to convince them He appeared to Moses. Aaron and Moses will be sent together.**

**Storyline Summary:**

- **Burning bush has Angel of Yahweh, God, and Yahweh in it**
- **Who are you? What is God’s name? “Ehyeh Asher Ehyeh” “Yahweh”**
- **Go, plunder the Egyptians**
- **How will the Hebrews believe me?**
- **3 miracles: staff into snake, leprous hand, Nile into Blood**
- **I’m not a good speaker, pick someone else**
- **Ok, really, I just don’t want to do it**
- **God gets angry**
- **God brings the older brother as the enforcer**
