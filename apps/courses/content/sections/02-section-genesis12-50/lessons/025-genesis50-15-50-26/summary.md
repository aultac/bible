

**Summary: Genesis 50:15-50:26**

**Title:** **Joseph assures his brothers they need not fear him with their father now dead. Joseph now dies and makes his family swear to carry his bones out when they leave Egypt someday.**

**Storyline Summary:**

- **Joseph’s brothers are afraid and send a letter to Joseph lying about Jacob instructing him to forgive his brothers.**
- **Joseph cries that his brother’s still don’t trust him.**
- **Joseph assures the brothers he will provide for them**
- **Joseph makes everyone swear to take his bones out of Egypt when they leave.**
