# 021_Genesis44-45:24

**Genesis 44:1-9 > The Stage is Set**

- BROTHERS THOUGHT IT WAS OVER: their first reaction had to be “**will this ever end**?”
- OVERCONFIDENT PROMISES: at this point you’d think the brother’s would not be so quick to suggest death to show their confidence. They needed Jesus advice: let your yes be yes and your no be no.
- TRUST EACH OTHER: No question among themselves that none of them did it. 100% faith. Did that faith break into suspicion after finding it in Benjamin’s sack? Did they think he might have actually done it?
- DIVINATION: What is it? Did Joseph talk to the spirit realm? Or did he just say it as part of the ruse? We know now this was standard practice in ancient Egypt. “Divination” means “**contacting the divine**”. “Water divination” involved, putting water in a bowl and either reading the reflection off of its surface like a crystal ball, or pouring a couple drops of oil on top and interpreting the resulting shapes. It does not appear that Joseph was a monotheist. It is possible, but the text says the cup was for divination, which meant Occult practices for contacting other gods who were over Egypt. God could have been the one responding, but it doesn’t say. It’s also possible that he’s lying and didn’t actually do divination, but the text doesn’t say it. The text says that he claimed to use it for divination.
- JIMMY STEWARD THE ACTOR: his acting here was superb. How many of us could have held a straight face after they said, kill whoever you find it with?

**Genesis 44:10-17 > Benjamin guilty, back to Pharoah**

- SUSPENSE DURING SEARCH: one at a time, no cup found. By the last one, we’re basically ready to go.
- REPEAT OF JOSEPH: they could send Benjamin back and head home. Problem solved. They would have done that with Joseph. But this time they go back.
- SECOND CHANCE TO BETRAY: Judah shows all the brothers had agreed to **one for all and all for one** with his first plea. Joseph says no need, you can all go home, just leave Benjamin here.
- HONOR YOUR MOTHER AND FATHER: the brothers are showing care for their father’s special love for Benjamin. Jacob has told them their whole lives that he didn’t want them and he didn’t want their mother. To have a father who doesn’t want you, and yet still **offer your life as a slave to ransom his favorite son is truly honoring your parents** as the 10 Commandments will eventually come to say. The fifth Commandments does not tell you that you have to love your parents, but it says you have to honor them for their position. If you do love your parents, consider yourself one of the blessed of the Earth. But even if you don’t love them, you have to honor them. You should talk to them at least once a week, even if they’re going to yell at you when you call. Just set the phone down for a minute if you have to. God will one day show them their error, so make sure you are doing your part. Because he will one day show you your errors with your children, too. **The brothers perfectly demonstrate that parental honor here.** And it is a beautiful thing.
- JUDAH’S DOUBLE MEANING: “God has found out the guilt of your servants”. They weren’t guilty about the cup, but they still knew **they were guilty about Joseph** all those years ago. So this sentence has two meanings at the same time in the story.

**Genesis 44:18-45:3 > Judah’s plea**

- ANSWER TO CAIN’S QUESTION: now we get in the narrative the answer to Cain’s question when God asked him where his brother Abel was. Cain replied “**Am I my brother’s keeper?”** Judah has realized that regardless of his brother’s favoritism status, he is indeed his brother’s keeper.
- NO EXCUSES: neither Judah nor the brothers make a single excuse before Joseph. Purely begging for mercy. This is a complete miracle: what human being in this situation would not immediately declare they were framed? Who would say “this happened last time and we brought all the money back!” Nothing like that here. **No appeal to extenuating circumstances, just pure self sacrifice by Judah. And Joseph knows this.** He was probably expecting them to at least claim innocence. Instead, Judah only says he cares about his father.
- SACRIFICE FOR HALF BROTHER: Judah offers to sacrifice himself to the “god” Pharoah for his half-brother, purely because “his father” asked him to. **Remind you of anyone else?**
- JOSEPH LEARNS WHY DAD DIDN’T LOOK FOR HIM: his dad thought he had been torn to pieces.
- BUT WHY DIDN’T JOSEPH CONTACT HIS DAD EARLIER? Why did he just choose to forget the family entirely even naming his son “Forgetting?” Jacob thought Joseph was dead, but Joseph knew Jacob was alive. If he had, his dreams would not have come true in order: first the 11 brothers bowing, the “sun moon and stars” bowing. God’s plan would not have been achieved in bringing everyone to Egypt. So maybe it’s just God’s provenance. Maybe he wanted to and God told him no.
- JOSEPH LEARNS HIS BROTHERS WERE VICTIMS TOO: he gets a small inkling that these 10 grown men were never wanted by their father. 40+ years, spent trying to earn his approval, only needs to be told you guys quit standing around and go get food while I keep the only remaining son of my only real wife here with me. Tragic.
- KNOWING PAIN BRINGS YOU CLOSER: Dennis Prager pointed out that when someone opens up to you about pain in their life, you almost inevitably feel closer to them, and they feel closer to you. Often times the people we don’t know well we imagine as having pain-free lives and it can lead us to despise them. One of the hardest things in life can be opening up to someone about pain and having them use that against you or mock you for it because you expect it to garner sympathy. Then there are those people who exploit this and over-share their problems to manipulate people into feeling sorry for them. **Joseph now as a 40 year-old adult, finally sees the pain in his brother’s lives and the closeness overwhelmed him with very loud weeping.**
- **3 INCLUSIOS: WORD DOC:**
  - JUDAH SLAVE SUGGESTIONS: Judah suggests slavery of younger favored brother Joseph, Judah suggests slavery for himself to Joseph to save younger favored brother Benjamin. **Hebrew word for “slave” and “servant” are the same word.**
  - JOSEPH CRYING: cries in the pit when they lose him, cries when they find him again.
  - 20 SILVERS: Nahum Sarne (Jewish commentator) points out that Joseph was sold for 20 pieces of silver. After that, until Joseph is revealed, there are 20 mentions of silver (kesef) leading to the brother’s almost enslavement and their reunion with Joseph (chapters 42-45). 20 silvers to sell, 20 silvers to reunite!

**Genesis 45:4-15 > Joseph revealed**

- STARTS SPEAKING HEBREW: Joseph kicked everybody out except his brothers, including one very important person: **his Hebrew interpreter**. if there was ever a situation for a double take in all of world literature, this is it. First, he’s crying uncontrollably, now this Egyptian starts speaking Hebrew. I wonder if they even understood the first words he spoke or if they were just in a daze?
- DISMAYED: how many of you remembered that this is the brother’s reaction? Dismay, or speechless, or astonished, or shocked. **Their reaction was not joy.** They never seem to fully trust Joseph. **When Jacob dies, they beg Joseph not to hurt them.** Genesis 50:15-21.
- ONLY JOSEPH AND BENJAMIN ARE HAPPY: Joseph weeps over them, and Benjamin is happy to see him, but the others only talk with him once they regain their faculties.
- JESUS’S ISRAELITE BROTHERS: to Joseph’s Israelite brothers who thought he was dead, **he is now risen from the dead** to and in a position of glory in a new Egyptian body such that they didn’t even recognize him when talking with him. But the brother’s don’t see the miracle and rejoice, instead they are dismayed. **Remind you of anyone?**
- BROTHERS LEARN 5 MORE YEARS OF FAMINE COMING: they didn’t know this before, they just came because they ran out of food.
- DETERMINISM VS FREE WILL: Joseph tells them don’t worry, God sent me here to save lives, including yours. This verse introduces one of life’s hardest questions to the world. **So are the brother’s responsible for their sin? Fate or Free Will?**
  - ATHEISTS ARE DETERMINISTS: Only a problem for religious people. Atheists have no choice but determinism. You are a bag of matter: the dominoes of history have fallen and caused all the molecules in your body to do what they are doing now. So you don’t know truth, and you have no choice but to do what you are doing now just as the dominoes in the line have no choice but to continue falling. But the atheist will argue with you as if they know real truth and you should know it too, **they do not argue as if they are just programmed to say what they are saying**. In other words, they are stuck trying to convince you that it is true that you cannot know truth. They have a double whammy because an evolved brain definitionally did not evolve to ascertain truth, it only can be said to have evolved to tilt toward survival. The atheist brain cannot be trusted to say anything true.
  - SECULAR CAN’T BLAME CRIMINALS OR SAINTS: This is why the more secular the society, the less likely they are to punish criminals. Because their crime is not their fault. But if so, the **good people do are not responsible for their good either.**
  - GOOD AND EVIL REQUIRE CHOICE: Without free will there is no good and evil, because there is no choice. If you give someone $10 because I am holding a gun to your head until you give them $10, then you are not being virtuous, you are just trying not to die. Goodness requires the choice to be good. Objective evil is easier, it clearly exists, in fact, it is one of the most common non-sequitors the atheist used to argue against the existence of God (babies die so God can’t exist). **So if evil exists, there must be free will**.
  - HOW TO RECONCILE: But how do we reconcile free will with God’s Sovereignty and Knowledge?
    - God controls everything
    - God knows everything
  - BALL AND WALL ANALOGY: Unstoppable ball and unbreakable wall: nonsensical, like saying “God can’t make a square circle”. It is undefined.
  - BUT WE HAVE BOTH: Reality seems to have both. The Bible seems to affirm both. **We don’t have language or concepts that are fully defined for both determinism and free will.**
  - WAYS PEOPLE RECONCILE: Line from determinism to free will, spaced along line
    - ATHEISM: God doesn’t exist, everything is determined.
    - COMPATIBILISM (free will and determinism are compatible): free to do what you want, but you don’t fully control what you want. Calvinism is a form of this.
    - LIBERTARIANISM (free to choose): You can choose independent of your desires, but God knows your choice. Arminianism is a form of this.
      - MOLINISM: God chose to create this world, knowing all the possible worlds.
    - OPEN THEISM: God chooses to not know what you will choose
- SO WHICH IS IT? **Anyone who tells you they know is lying to you.**  Thankfully, **our opinion on this actually doesn’t matter.** Regardless of whether things are determined, or we have free will, our understanding of the fact does not change it. Y**ou and I will not be on the throne at judgment day, deciding. God will.** And what we do know of that day is that we will all be incredibly amazed at the Justice of God and we will sing his praise. It is just not our call.
