The sum of the factors is always 17, which is the number of years that Jacob lived in Egypt. I don’t know what it means, except to show that God has designed everything on the path to salvation.

**Genesis 48:8-20 > Jacob blesses Ephraim and Manasseh**

- VOICE IS THE VOICE OF JACOB, BUT THE HANDS ARE THE HANDS OF ESAU: Jacob’s eyes are dim. He gives the birthright to the younger. In the next verse, in fact, he almost asks who are you? Just ask his dad asked him when he was pretending to be Esau. **Now you think it really is Ephraim who the promise will flow through!**
- ANGEL OF YAHWEH: “the angel who redeemed me from all evil”: did the angel do it or did God?
- GOD MAKE YOU AS EPHRAIM AND MANASSEH: every Friday evening Jewish fathers to this day place their hands on the heads of their sons and quote this verse. For the daughters, they say God made you like Sarah, Rachel, Leah, and Rebecca. Why the matriarchs for the girls but not the patriarchs for the boys (Abraham, Isaac, Jacob)? Rabbi Joseph Hertz, in his Orthodox commentary on the Torah, says “To this day, every pious Jewish father on Sabbath Eve places his hand on his son and blesses him in the words ‘God make you like Ephraim and Manasseh’. Ephraim and Manasseh would not barter away their Jewishness for the most exalted social position or the most enviable political career in the Egyptian state. **They voluntarily gave up their place in the higher Egyptian aristocracy and openly identified themselves with their ‘alien’ kinsmen, the despised shepherd immigrants**. Every Jewish parent may well pray that their child would show dedication to their father and their father’s God as Ephraim and Manasseh did.”
- STRANGE CEREMONY: why would Jacob do this, elevate Joseph’s sons basically to the position of being his brothers? **In order to double Joseph’s inheritance, as a tribute to Rachel**, who would have had more children in his mind if she hadn’t died with Benjamin. That’s why his mind wanders to Rachel seemingly out of the blue.
- JACOB, THE PROPHET: this clearly means beyond just the blessing of a grandfather to a grandson or a son, this is prophecy. His dad, Isaac’s blessing was unclear if it was totally prophecy or just a father‘s blessing to his sons. This one is a combination of both, but there is clearly prophecy here.
- HOW DOES IT PAN OUT?
  - Ephraim early lead: Numbers 1 census right after exodus, Ephraim 40,500, Manasseh 32,200
  - Manasseh pulls ahead: Numbers 26 census 40 years later Ephraim 52,700, Ephraim 32,500
  - Joshua is Ephraimite
  - Manasseh gets two huge territories, much more than Ephraim, but not easily cleared of forests and Canaanites.
  - Jeroboam son of Nebat is Ephramite. Takes northern 10 tribes after Solomon’s son screws up, sets golden calves.
  - Samaria is capital of the north, located in Manasseh’s territory.
  - Ephraim becomes the most mighty of all the northern tribes because of original Jeroboam leadership. Later in the prophets, the name Ephraim becomes synonymous with all of Israel.
  - Ephraim ends up in civil war with Judah and Benjamin. The promise will flow through Judah, the one who showed contrition and offered himself as a sacrifice for Benjamin. In the end, Ephraim became much greater than Manasseh, and they try to destroy Judah and Benjamin, but Judah stands with his brother Benjamin and the Assyrians end up wiping out all northern Israel.

Either he fought to enlarge it, or he is counting Simeon and Levi’s conquest as his own, even though he despises them for it. There’s no other fighting recorded that takes place during Jacob’s lifetime.
- WHO ARE THE AMORITES?:
  - \~2600BC: NOAH’S GRANDSON CANAAN: Ham’s son, Canaan, was cursed after Ham “uncovered his father’s nakedness”. Amorites listed as his descendants.
  - 2500BC: Amorites appear in Sumerian Cuneiform texts as “westerners”
  - 2000BC: Abraham’s friend Mamre (“Oaks of Mamre”) was an Amorite.
  - <SAME>: God tells Abraham he won’t get promised land permanently yet because “iniquity of the Amorites is not yet complete”. Genesis 15:16. Then the fire covenant passing between the split halves of the sacrificed animals that night.
  - 1800BC: Jacob refers to Amorites as sort of catch-all for Canaanites
  - 1446BC: Exodus forward uses “Amorites” as “Canaanites”
- DIFFERENCE WITH ALL OTHER GROUP HISTORIES: so Jacob fought battles and acquired land. We NEVER hear of it in the story when it happened. **All other histories of peoples and kings are obsessed with pointing out every military victory and glory.** Jacob’s story only mentions it in passing at the end of his life, with zero detail. As we get into Exodus through Joshua, which takes on a very conquest-flavored picture, remember that **Genesis showed us the point was not conquest, it was goodness and God’s promise.** Jacob, for all his faults, did not revel in his conquests.
- JOSEPH’S TOMB: From https://www.chabad.org/library/article_cdo/aid/588228/jewish/Josephs-Tomb-Shechem.htm: In 1867 Mark Twain visited Shechem. He described this visit in his diary which was later published. The following are some interesting excerpts:

 "At two o'clock we stopped to lunch and rest at ancient Shechem, between the historic Mounts of Gerizim and Ebal, where in the old times the books of the law, the curses and the blessings, were read from the heights to the Jewish multitudes below.

 "About a mile and a half from Shechem we halted at the base of Mount Ebal before a little square area, enclosed by a high stone wall, neatly whitewashed. Across one end of this enclosure is a tomb . . . It is the tomb of Joseph. No truth is better authenticated than this.

 "Few tombs on earth command the veneration of so many races and men of diverse creeds as this of Joseph. Samaritan and Jew, Muslim and Christian alike, revere it, and honor it with their visits.  Regarding the city's Jewish community he writes:

 "For thousands of years this clan have dwelt in Shechem under strict taboo and having little commerce or fellowship with their fellowmen of any religion or nationality. For generations they have not numbered more than one or two hundred, but they still adhere to their ancient faith and maintain their ancient rites and ceremonies. Talk of family and old descent! … This handful of old first families of Shechem…can name their fathers straight back without a flaw for thousands \[of years\]…. I found myself gazing at any straggling scion of this strange race with a riveted fascination, just as one would stare at a living mastodon or a megatherium…"

**Genesis 49:1-27 > All Son’s Blessings**

- POETRY: this is poetry, so recall that the purpose of prose is to replicate an idea or thing from brain to brain or mind to mind. The purpose of poetry is to communicate with the soul. It is the author’s attempt to recreate an experience in the soul of the hearer. It uses imagery, sounds, and an appeal to shared experiences elsewhere to attempt to get you to feel something. So when you see poetry and listen to it, ask yourself, what experience or feeling is the author going for here? This is why Biblical poetry is so hard to grasp: we do not have many shared experiences with people 4000 years ago. For example, **when they refer to a “wild donkey”, we don’t get the same feelings they did.**  They relied on donkeys for work, for travel, for carrying their burdens. They were their lifelines, a part of their everyday life from sunrise to sundown. Even worse, **some of the Hebrew words in this section only appear here and nowhere else**, so it is hard to know what they actually mean.
- SECULAR PEOPLE CLAIM THIS WAS WRITTEN LATER: that’s how so many prophecies came true. But if it was later, when Levi was the most elevated tribe, the priestly class, why would Levi have been shown so negatively? What is not in dispute is the accuracy of the predictions.
- RUEBEN: Jacob’s Revenge
- SIMEON AND LEVI: Dennis Prager points out that one key difference between good men and bad men is how they act when they are angry. Too many people today give violence a pass because the perpetrator had a grievance: they are angry so their overreaction is forgiven. **Jacob does the opposite: doubles down on the condemnation precisely because of how they acted when they were angry.** Predictions came true: Levi had no land, but were priests (a blessing), and Simeon was decimated by plague in Numbers and was given land within Judah, eventually dispersing throughout during civil war.
- JUDAH: best blessing except for Joseph. Why does Jacob give this blessing? What did Judah do that earned Jacob’s respect? **He pledged to bring back Benjamin, and he did, even after pledging to sacrifice his own life to do it.**
  - SCEPTER SHALL NOT PASS: Jews and Christians it means the divine right to rule stays with Judah until the Messiah comes. They just disagree on whether Jesus was that Messiah.
- ZEBULUN: Jumps to son number 10, but continues with Leah’s line after Judah, although Issachar came first. “Haven for ships” would suggest Zebulun’s territory touched the water, so some maps have it stretching from the Mediterranean Sea to the Sea of Galilee, but the map I am using does not. I do not know the reasoning behind the maps.
- ISSACHAR: nobody knows what this means. But Issachar is end of Leah’s children in this list, although he was older than Zebulun. Maybe this is just the order they were standing around the bed.
- DAN: the name Dan means “judge”, first child of Bilhah, Rachel’s maid, when Rachel said God had judged her and granted her a son through Bilhah.   **Viper and serpent could refer to Dan being the first to worship other gods in Judges 18**: they steal another guy’s gods and his priest, then the massacre an innocent town, and have Jonathan son of Gershom son of Moses as their priest.
- WAIT FOR SALVATION? Why does he say this? Is he just blurting something out as he approaches death? Is he having a vision that is leading to these prophecies? Does he see what Dan becomes in that vision and he cries out in anguish?
- GAD: firstborn of Zilpah, Leah’s maid. His tribe is on the Eastern border, East of Jordan, and they do become border warriors. The final overcoming seems to be when they destroyed the Hagrites under King Saul in 1 Chronicles 5.
- ASHER: this is the blessing you want. You want to be Asher. This came true, they got the best dirt in Canaan. They supplied food to Hiram King of Tyre (royal dainties).
- NAPHTALI: female deer with pretty babies, not exactly masculine. I don’t know what it means.
- JOSEPH: Jacob praises God as the source of all his blessings, and he passes all those blessings onto Joseph.
- BENJAMIN: kind of like Naphtali, a short cryptic comparison to an animal, but much stronger.
- ASK GOD SOMEDAY: most of these are cryptic enough we don’t really know for sure what was meant. We can find out from God someday.
- WHAT IS MISSING IN THESE BLESSINGS? It appears that Jacob never learned what the brothers did to Joseph. If he had, how could he not have mentioned it in any of the brother’s parting words?

**Genesis 50:2-3 > Embalmed Jacob and Egyptian Weeping**

- WHY DID EGYPTIANS EMBALM? They believed after death your soul in the spiritual world would come back at night to inhabit your earthly body to refresh and recharge. If your body decayed or was destroyed, your spirit would lose its “anchor” and it would die a second time, “the second death”. Spell 175 in “the book of the dead” was titled “Spell for not dying a second time.” That terrified them more than anything.
- WHY DID JOSEPH EMBALM JACOB? He used “physicians” not “priests”: he had to travel with the body 17 days. Egypt mourned him like a Pharoah for 70 days. It’s hot in Egypt. Bodies last a few hours. Trust me the farmer, I know.

**Genesis 50:4-14 > Funeral Procession through Threshing floor of Atad**

- “SIT SHIVA”: when they close relative to someone Jewish dies, they “sit Shiva“ which means “sit seven“. They leave their regular life for seven days and sit in their house while the people they know come and sit with them. This shows that practice is an ancient: that is what Joseph and crew have done here. They sat in the morning for seven days.
- BEYOND THE JORDAN: it is on the Egyptian side of the Jordan. Scholars arguing for the Mosaic authorship of the Torah say this phrase shows Moses wrote it from the Eastern side of the Jordan, since it was “beyond the Jordan.”
