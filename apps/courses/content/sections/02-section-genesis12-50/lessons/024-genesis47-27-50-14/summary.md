

**Summary: Genesis 47:27-50:14**

**Title:** **Jacob blesses Manasseh and Ephraim as his owns sons, Jacob calls his 12 sons together to give them blessings/prophecies of what will happen in the days to come, Jacob dies and they travel back to Canaan for the burial.**

**Storyline Summary:**

- **Jacob makes Joseph swear to bury him in Canaan in the cave that Abraham bought from Ephron the Hittite**
- **Jacob claims Manasseh and Ephraim as his own sons**
- **Then he asks them “who are you?”**
- **Jacob blesses Joseph through his sons**
- **Jacob puts Ephraim ahead of Manasseh**
- **He gave extra portions to Joseph**
- **Jacob blesses/curses all the other sons around the bed before his death**
- **Right to rule stays with Judah until Christ**
- **Jacob dies, is embalmed, head back to Canaan for burial with a big parade.**
