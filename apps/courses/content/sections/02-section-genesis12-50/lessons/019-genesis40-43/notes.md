# 019_Genesis40-41:45

**Genesis 40:1-8 > baker and cupbearer**

- CAT ALWAYS LANDS ON HIS FEET: Joseph ends up in charge wherever he goes. He had no formal training. Just a natural doer. I suspect Potiphar put in a good word for him?
- KINDNESS OF JOSEPH: he noticed their moods and cared enough to ask them about it. Women do this naturally, they have a built-in radar that is **constantly subconsciously estimating the mood of every person in the room**. Men do not. Joseph had to be very intentional to even notice.

**Genesis 40:9-end > dreams and fulfillment**

- SAME DREAM AS A FRIEND: imagine coming in this morning and talking to someone and find out they had almost the same dream as you…
- A LOT OF THREES: cupbearer’s dream has 3 branches, 3 verbs for vine growth, 3 days, 3 pharaohs, 3 cups, 3 mentions of himself
- THE POOR BAKER: we don’t know if he was a scoundrel. But this was hard for anybody. And Joseph doesn’t sugarcoat it.
- FIRST SUPERNATURAL GIFT OF THE SPIRIT: is interpretation of dreams. Later, we get Moses, prophets, Jesus. Dreams is first though.
- HOW DID THIS WORK? Did God whisper it to him? Was Joseph in a trance? Did he just “know”? Hard to believe he just “knew” but still always attributed it to God. Was he just sitting there like a third-rate psychic? Whatever he does, people seem to believe him explicitly now, where his brothers did not. Does that remind you of anyone else? Mark 6:4 “And Jesus said to them, “**A prophet is not without honor, except in his hometown and among his relatives and in his own household.**” A**nd he could do no mighty work there**, except that he laid his hands on a few sick people and healed them. And he marveled because of their unbelief.”
- JOSEPH ASKS CUPBEARER FOR RELEASE INSTEAD OF GOD: **Joseph is never recorded as having prayed to God.**  Given all the times he would have felt despair, this is a significant omission from the storyline. You don’t get the sense that he did not pray, or that he did not trust in God, but it is an odd omission. **He didn’t sit around and wait for a miracle here.** He took initiative and asked someone for help. Story of man waiting for God to save him in flood on house roof, denying help from neighbor in canoe, police in motorboat, coast guard in helicopter.
- DON’T PATTERN PATRIARCHS: just because Joseph isn’t recorded as praying doesn’t mean you shouldn’t pray. The patriarchs’ lives aren’t here for you to emulate, they are here for you to learn from and avoid their mistakes. Don’t avoid praying because Joseph didn’t pray. Jesus told us to pray and ask for what we want repeatedly. Paul told us to pray without ceasing. Jesus told us we can’t pray “wrong”: we always pray wrong but the Holy Spirit fixes it before God. Don’t interpret dreams thinking God gave you the answer. Don’t sacrifice your child assuming God will stop you. Don’t marry two women and hate one and love the other. Don’t throw your brother in a cistern as a favor to him so he becomes Pharoah of Egypt. **The patriarchs lived particular lives with particular circumstances that God dealt with in particular ways that do not always generalize to your life. You are not Joseph. You are Cain.**
- CUPBEARER UNMOVED BY JOSEPH’S CLAIM OF INNOCENCE: **the hardest thing about being wrongly convicted is that innocence is the most common refrain of the criminal.**  “ I didn’t do it. I’m innocent. They framed me.” The boy who cried wolf: when it does actually happen, no one will listen to you. But God knows.
- ONLY BIRTHDAY IN OLD TESTAMENT: is Pharoah’s birthday mentioned here.

**Genesis 41:1-13 > Joseph remembered**

- CARNIVOROUS COWS: not the Western Disney version of animals. Blood, gore, teeth, etc. More like Wingfeather Saga Toothy Cows. Would scare anyone.
- MAGICIANS: who are these guys, and why would the pharaoh think they could interpret his dream? Did none of them even offer a guess?
- PICTURE:
- DREAM INTERPRETATION WAS A PROFESSION: **Papyrus Chester Beatty III** scroll from about 1200 BC is in the British museum. It is a dream interpretation manual: sets of symbols from dreams and what they mean.
  - If you see yourself, eating bread: Good, good things are coming to you
  - If you see a crocodile: bad, danger from your enemies are from the water
  - If you see a dead person: good, you will have long life

- RITUALS: the study of the books also had rituals like burning incense with chants and “spells” to Thoth, patron God of wisdom, writing, and magic, or Bes the God of sleep who everybody had a little idol of by their bed to ward off nightmares.
- MAGICK: sounds like Taroh cards and psychics, right? This is what the world believed, and it is what New Age and Eastern religions believe today. Another name for it is the Occult. The Occult calls their spells and rituals to affect the world and interpret dreams “magick” with a “k” to distinguish it from what we call “magic” which is all illusion. They believe the spiritual world is affecting the real world and that the magic is real. **They believe that the spiritual world adheres to rules and laws just like the physical world, and if you discover those rules and laws then you can harness the power of the spiritual world for yourself.** This is what a “spell” or “incantation” is: performing an action that forces the spiritual world to react in some way. Do you believe that? Do you believe psychics know things they shouldn’t? Does the Bible? **Read Deuteronomy 18:9-14** (and Leviticus 19). **Read Ephesians 6:10-13. Did God prohibit sorcery, mediums, and psychics because they don’t work or because they are contacting demons?**
- CS LEWIS ERRORS ABOUT THE DEVILS: If you have never studied the Occult, they can seem fascinating from the outside. You can learn a lot by understanding the tactics of the enemy. But you will lose yourself in it. The enemy is happy for us to study them instead of God. As C. S. Lewis put it in the forward of The Screwtape Letters, “**There are two equal and opposite errors into which our race can fall about the devils. One is to disbelieve in their existence. The other is to believe, and to feel an excessive and unhealthy interest in them.**” This comes from a man who, while an atheist, ended up participating in Occult rituals, believing them to be fake fun ways to spend a night, until he spent 14 days holding down a man writhing on the ground screaming that demons were tearing him apart and that he was falling into hell.
- DOES THE BIBLE SAY THE DREAM INTERPRETERS WERE CHARLATANS? No, notice that it says they didn’t give Pharaoh an interpretation for his dream at all. If anything, it gives them credibility. **It does not tell you that dream interpretation is fake.**
- ENVIRONMENT OF JOSEPH’S STORY: “dream interpreter” was one of the highest respected professions, like a professor at college. They believed it was 100% real and accurate, not fake. And they were probably somewhat right: they were contacting demons. But dream interpreters had to study books, make inferences from them, and do rituals like sleeping in the temple overnight. Joseph stands before Pharaoh and consults nothing but his God. **This is why they believed him: fake dream interpreters were like fake doctors or engineers would be to us.** It just doesn’t happen. **This was their science.** If you had a dream that could not be interpreted, you should be studied as a research subject.

**Genesis 41:14-24 > Pharoah’s dream**

- IMAGINE THE CONTRAST: years in the dark dungeon to immediately washed, shaved, and yanked into Pharoah’s court in a matter of an hour or two. Squinting. Nervous that this is your only chance.
- STILL PUTS GOD FIRST: **Joseph had every reason to sell himself** before Pharoah as the one interpreting the dream. But he does not. He says “I don’t know anything, only God can tell you.”

**Genesis 41:25-36 > Joseph interprets**

- DUAL DREAMS: now we learn why all the dreams in this story come in 2’s: it means God has decreed it, it will happen. Parallelism in real life. This is why Jacob considered it when Jacob told him his second dream.

**Genesis 41:37-45 > Joseph takes power**

- NOBODY ASKED WHY HE WAS IN THE DUNGEON: wouldn’t this be your first question before putting this imprisoned foreigner in charge of your entire kingdom? Do you suppose Pharaoh talked to Potiphar while Joseph was being prepared? And Potiphar said Joseph was the best administrator he had ever seen? Or did the cup bear put in a good word for him?
- RECAP LAST 13 YEARS: cistern, prisoner all the way to Egypt, 11 years slave to Potiphar instead of in the Turqoise or gold mines, dungeon for 2 years, shaved and dressed and yanked into light of Pharoah’s court, then made basically Pharoah himself, married to high priest’s daughter. He thought he was dead, but now he is king.
- “ON” IS NOT A GOD: it is a city (Heliopolis). The city where Ra the sun God started his circuit across the sky.
