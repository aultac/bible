

**Summary: Genesis 46:31-47:26**

**Title:** **Jacob's family settles in Goshen with Pharaoh’s permission as shepherds while famine continues, with Joseph exchanging food for everyone’s livestock and buying all their land for Pharoah, except for the Land of the priests.**

**\-- OR –**

**Pharaoh gets extremely rich and Joseph sets his family up for success.**

**Storyline Summary:**

- **Israelites become in charge all of Pharoah’s livestock**
- **Jacob is 130 years old, Pharoah asks “How old are you”**
- **All the livestock of Egypt and Canaan become Pharoah’s and therefore under control of Israelites.**
- **Egyptians sold themselves and their land to Pharoah to get food**
